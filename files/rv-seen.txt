whats-new entry: "NFL My Career: your rival finally plays your position. In NFL My Career the rival from your draft class was always drafted at your position, but his season line never really was. A corner's rival printed sacks. A kicker's rival had no attempts and no long. A tight end's rival put up a wide receiver's numbers, and every one of them played a 17 game year even in a 16 game season. Corners, linebackers and edge rushers almost never won a year against him because of it. Now his season is made by the same thing that makes yours, so he prints the same line you do at your position, over as many games as that season really had. Who had the better year is read straight off the two lines on your screen: yards, touchdowns and interceptions for a quarterback, rushing yards, touchdowns and catches for a back (each catch counts as eight yards), catches, yards and touchdowns for receivers and tight ends, and for a defender or a kicker the numbers his own line shows. A linebacker's or a corner's forced fumbles and a kicker's misses are not on the line, so they do not count. The All-Pro Team card knows both seasons now: it tells you if you both made the first team, only you, or only him, and at quarterback, running back, tight end and kicker there is one spot, so it is never both. The old All Star Week card, about a roster the game never picked, is The Charity Weekend now. On a save you already have, the head to head record stays as it is and the new way starts with your next season."
