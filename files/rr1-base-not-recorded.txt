We ship a lot. If something looks off after an update, hit the Report a bug button in the footer and we will get on it. October 2026 Flags in Footle, and six countries find their home. In Footle a player's country used to show as a bare word in three spots: the example in How to play, the line under the answer in a five-puzzle run, and the Nation box in the player details. All three carry the flag now. And in Club Manager the nationality filter on the Market had a leftover group called Elsewhere. Niger, Namibia, Mauritius, South Sudan, Turkmenistan and French Guiana were sitting in it because nobody had told the game where they play their football. Each one now sits under its own confederation with everybody else, and Elsewhere is gone.
NFL, MLB and NHL My Career: the roster card stops flipping a coin, and the Trophy Case counts everything. Two things the NBA career just got, now in the other three. The rival card about the All-Star roster used to flip a coin, so it could say you made it in a year your own season card said you did not. Now it reads your own seasons. In MLB and the NHL it turns up when you make the All-Star roster, or when you drop off it a year after making it. In the NFL it is about the first team All-Pro, because that is the honour the game really hands out (it never picked a Pro Bowl). It says nothing about whether your rival made it, because his season is not tracked that closely in those three yet. The NFL's joint practice card lost its coin as well: nobody keeps a score in camp, so the card stopped naming a winner. And the Trophy Case tile on the hub now counts every award in the case behind it. A Gold Glove, a batting title, a Calder, a Selke or a Rookie of the Year used to leave the tile short, and a career with only those read Empty. Seasons you already played keep everything they had, and a coin card you were looking at when this landed just closes.
Club Manager: the real 2026/27 Premier League fixture list. Another one you asked for. Start a new career in today's Premier League and your first season follows the real fixture list: who you play in each of the 38 rounds, and whether you are home or away. Everton open at home to Crystal Palace and then go to Bournemouth, the way the real season is laid out. Only the order and the venues are real. The dates on your calendar and every result are still the game's own, and the cups are drawn the way they always were. Other leagues, older eras, custom clubs, edited worlds, saves you already have and every season after the first keep the game's own generated fixtures. When the real list is in use the Calendar says so and links the two places we checked it against, and the help has the details.
Soccer Career: your derby history. Career Stats in Soccer Career has a Derby history button now. Pick a season and you get every rival your club met that year and each derby your save kept: home or away, the score with the home club first, and whether you played in it. Or pick a rival and see your whole career against that club, every meeting with the season and the club you were at. The club's record and your own appearances are counted apart, two spells in one year stay separate, and a season from before derbies were recorded says so and makes nothing up.
Soccer Career: compare two seasons, and see when you were out. Ratings can now put any two of your senior seasons side by side: appearances, the numbers that matter for your position, your overall and your match rating, and how much each one moved from the first season to the second. Loan spells and seasons where you never got on the pitch can be picked too. A second view lists the injuries your save recorded, with the weeks and how serious they were, and the matches you missed through suspension. Where an older season did not keep something it says Not recorded and guesses nothing.
Soccer Career: a trophy opens the run that won it. Tap a European title or another club title in the Trophy Cabinet and you get the campaign your save kept: the first stage games, every knockout leg, home or away, the scores, your goals in those games, and how a tie was settled where the save kept it. All wins takes you back to the tile you opened. A title from an older save that did not keep its games says so and shows nothing made up, and domestic titles look the way they did.
Soccer Career: story chapters, a smaller role, and goal milestones. Career Story opens as chapters now, with Previous and Next to move between them, and All seasons takes you back to the tile you came from. Taking a smaller role from a new manager now means something: four fewer planned league games next season at that club, so a season of 30 becomes 26 before injuries and bans. It lasts one season, and a move, a loan or retiring clears it. Your season summary marks the season you pass 100, 200, 300 or 500 senior club goals, with your tally before and after. Academy and international goals do not count toward it, and it comes with no bonus. And on a phone, Phone and Training sit in a row above your player, with the ? button clear of them.
Soccer Career: last season counts, a preseason plan, and a youngster to mentor. Three things now shape your next season. Form: a rating of 7.6 or better across at least 10 league games at the same club adds two games to next season's selection, and 6.4 or worse takes two away, so a 24 game season becomes 26 after a 7.8 year. Preseason: pick development (+1 to your position skill after growth, 3 points more injury risk), recovery (1 less skill, 4 points less risk) or balanced, so a 20% injury risk becomes 23% or 16%. And when the Youth Mentor event comes up, saying yes starts one made up academy player for you to bring through: each season with ten senior appearances at the club you share moves it on a year, three years finish it, and a move, a loan or retiring ends it. The squad sheet tells you when form moved your games, and each of the three has a tile with its rules.
Soccer Career: a target for next season, and a record book. Before a season you can pick one personal challenge: beat your own best club season for goals, assists, clean sheets or rating, whichever fit your position. Your best is 12 goals, so the target is 13. Hit it and you bank +1 training with next season's growth. It does not change who gets picked or what you score, a serious injury or a year without a game interrupts it, and moving clubs cancels it. And there is a record book: your best seasons across all club competitions and every club spell you have had, read straight from the seasons your save holds. Tap a best to see the season it came from.
Dart Draft: legends, wonderkids and wildcards change from throw to throw. Those three zones used to show the same top names for a position every time (eight legends, eight wonderkids, ten wildcards). Now each shows eight: the best three are always there and the other five are drawn from everyone else who fits, so the tile you want might be one you have never been offered. The same name never shows twice on one throw, and a country is read all the way down now, not just its first 120 players. Throw a few.
Stat Detective: what the years mean, and better reports. The rules and each case now carry one line on what Career span and Career franchises cover: every NBA season we have on file for the player, short stints included, with years given as the year a season ended. And if you report a problem from a case, the report now tells us which case you were on and what you had guessed, so we can find it. Open a case.
NBA My Career: the numbers tell the truth now. Stat lines in NBA My Career look like the league. A starter rated in the low 80s scores about 16 a night where the game used to hand him about 20, wings stopped passing like point guards, points go to one decimal, and every season you play from here also keeps your minutes, steals and blocks. All-Star selections exist now, with two new badges (23 in the Trophy Case). The awards add up too: MVP only goes to an All-NBA First Team player on a playoff team, the Rookie of the Year is always on the All-Rookie First Team, All-Defensive goes to actual defenders, and from 2023-24 the 65 game rule is on. A throwback career plays 66 games in 2011-12 and 72 in 2020-21, like the league did, and a trade demand can no longer land you on the team you are already on. Your rival is on the same numbers too: his season line comes from the same place yours does, so when the game says who had the better year, the two lines on the screen back it up. The All-Star card goes by who really made the roster, a near tie tells you who actually leads the head to head (that one was off in all four My Careers), and the Trophy Case tile counts every award, so an All-Rookie or All-Defensive season no longer leaves it reading Empty. Seasons you already played keep the lines they had. The rules and a worked MVP case are on the ? button.
