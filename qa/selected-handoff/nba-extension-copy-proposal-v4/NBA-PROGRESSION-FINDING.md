# NBA season-twenty source follow-up

Source reviewed: the exact b20 engine/UI retained in PR 402's qualified original, the selected 16-case test source, and ExtensionCard copy v2. No application or test was executed in this review.

The six new PR 402 cases create twenty fictional season rows and explicitly set age, year, rating, health and accolades. They then exercise real board market and one-season transitions. This is useful boundary coverage, but it does not prove that twenty real season simulations and their intervening decisions arrive at a playable state.

The actual board opens extension/free-agency decisions before simulation (UsCareerBoard.tsx lines 574-594), then runs camp, simSeason and progress (598-604), and checks retirement before the next offseason (621-627). The NBA engine appends each played row (nbaMyCareer.ts line 888), increments age/year and decrements contract years once (911-913). Retirement is rating at most 66, age at least 41, or 21 played rows (1115-1116). A fresh career starts at age 19-21 (332); a player who starts at 21 can legitimately retire after twenty seasons. This source inspection found no universal missing-offer lock.

The smallest meaningful missing check is one deterministic fresh zero-season career driven through the real board's Play, contract, offseason, retirement-talk and reveal actions until twenty actual season rows have been generated. Keep the actual random stream and chosen actions, every generated season row, contract changes and saved phase. Do not fill rows, force ages/ratings/health, rewrite contract years or disable retirement to reach twenty. Pick and retain a bounded reproducible legal progression that reaches the boundary; a run retiring earlier is evidence of its own outcome, not coverage of this report.

At the actual 19-to-20 boundary and next attempted season, verify the visible action matches the genuine saved state: an unexpired deal continues, a nullable extension can be declined to play, an expired active deal opens signable offers, or an ended career has a retirement outcome. Reload that genuinely reached boundary and verify exact saved terms/history without a redraw or extra played season. The existing fixture tests remain separate and do not need duplicating. The original reporter's save is still unavailable, so even a successful progression would not establish that their particular issue was reproduced.
