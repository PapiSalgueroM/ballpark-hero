# Historical opponent names in Soccer Career

The 2023 Premier League ledger contains 20 members and 19 canonical club names. Its remaining member is Luton Town. This change connects that verified identity to the unique unnamed replay slot, so fixture, match and table use the same name.

Membership was checked against [RSSSF](https://www.rsssf.org/tablese/eng2024.html) and the [Premier League](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4016996) on 2026-10-14. Match order, scores and table positions in the career replay are simulated. No historical result or rating is imported.

The existing season ledger stays unchanged. The generator adds a separate small display lookup and checks it before writing either output. The Season Centre uses a labels-only presentation copy after derivation. No career field, league pool, game, player total, moment, random draw or saved schema changes.

The lookup requires the exact season and league, one unused verified member, one unnamed slot and complete opponent coverage. Wrong scope, foreign or duplicate labels, multiple unknown slots and incomplete frames return no mapping. Other historical gaps and synthetic clubs are outside this change.
