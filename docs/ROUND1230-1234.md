## Soccer Career

Objective: answer for the season you actually played, then live with the promise you made.

An optional Press room tile opens after a professional season with appearances. Answers are available on the playing screen; recorded history stays readable during summaries and after retirement. The question follows the saved club, position and season facts. Eight topics and rotating questions cover titles, injury, selection, moves, form, discipline, contract years and ordinary seasons. No ballot result is read or revealed.

Choose a calm answer (morale +3, popularity -2), take responsibility (popularity +3, morale -3, credibility +2), or promise a better next season (morale -4). A promise captures its position-specific target and club. The next actual playing season at that club settles it once. Met: popularity +6, morale +3, credibility +8. Missed: popularity -6, morale -3, credibility -8. A club move or interrupted year closes the promise without a reward or penalty. The history keeps the question, answer, target and outcome. Reading, closing and reloading give nothing.

Controls: compact tile, one question, three answers, history and reopenable help with a worked example. Back stays available, actions have 44px targets and opening preserves the page and focus. This adds no random draws. Old saves with no press state remain neutral.

## Four US careers

Objective: choose a contract with the same offers and completed negotiations after a reload.

Save a complete extension or free-agency window, bound to sport, year, current team and contract terms. Opening and pushing write one atomic snapshot. Reload restores the snapshot without rebuilding offers. Signing, declining or playing past the year clears the relevant talk. Invalid or stale optional data is discarded alone. Existing save failure and retry behavior stays in charge.

A push uses the original negotiation engine and its original random draws. The wheel shows the exact probabilities of raised, held and withdrawn outcomes, including incumbent and final-offer protection. Spin and Skip present the already committed result. Continue only acknowledges it. Reduced motion shows the final state without animation. No wheel can redraw an offer or alter an outcome.

Offer comparison reads annual pay, total pay, length and roster quality from the actual held offers. Picking a comparison priority signs nothing and changes no offer. Roster quality is a simulation rating, not a promised championship or playing role. Farewell seasons retain one-year deals.
