## What managers actually work on

FIFA's microcycle example connects the previous match review, the next opponent, training objectives and player needs. It is an example of planning, not a universal Monday-to-Sunday timetable. Fixture congestion changes the time available. [David Aznar on the microcycle,2023-03-30](https://www.fifatrainingcentre.com/en/environment/expert-knowledge/david-aznar-on-planning-the-microcycle.php).

UEFA's professional-coach syllabus covers staff leadership, individual development, team preparation, club accountability and balancing players' professional and private lives. It asks coaches to work with analysts, fitness, medical and other specialists, and to prepare and review matches with their staff. [UEFA Pro syllabus](https://editorial.uefa.com/resources/0267-11ea4a73c70c-394e8502245f-1000/uefa_pro_diploma_2020_en.pdf). These are role responsibilities, not evidence that every head coach personally negotiates transfers or controls every club department.

Analysis supports practical decisions using tactical footage, match data and reports. Our advice must therefore explain a real saved fact and offer an existing legal action, rather than grant invisible information or an automatic bonus. [UEFA performance analysis](https://www.uefa.com/development/performance-analysis/about-performance-analysis/).

Returning from injury is a staged, individualized transition back to team training. FIFA's technical sessions are examples for practitioners, not medical prescriptions. In-game readiness and recovery choices must use the recorded injury, communicate uncertainty and preserve engine availability; an event cannot promise to heal a player. [FIFA return-from-injury introduction,2024-05-07](https://www.fifatrainingcentre.com/en/practice/returning-from-injury/colbert---returning-from-injury-introduction.php).

Players also fit education and preparation for a later career around football. Player associations offer study, personal-development and transition support. This supports a grounded off-pitch course or planning arc, without inventing a qualification or guaranteeing a future job. [FIFPRO development](https://www.fifpro.org/en/what-we-do/development-and-impact), [career-transition support](https://www.fifpro.org/en/articles/2019/10/mind-the-gap-take-control-of-your-next-career).

## What counts as finished

1. A new signed-out player can understand the objective, create a career, make an initial decision and reach the first playable fixture or season. Help includes rules and a worked example and remains reopenable.
2. The hub clearly identifies the next legal action. Pending selection, contracts, medical work, transfers and events cannot strand progression. Back, neutral delegation and quick advance are available where the loop permits them.
3. A choice tells the player what changes, its cost and uncertainty. The resulting saved state matches those words. Training respects development headroom; an interaction cannot invent minutes, heal an injury or guarantee selection.
4. A promised follow-up arrives after the relevant actual advance. It reads the earlier choice, closes once and remains stable after pending and resolved saves reload. Declining a choice is a valid path with honest consequences.
5. Finance, contract, calendar, match and season-history screens agree. Transfers preserve player agency. Goals, opponents, competition membership and honours come from the engine's reconciled record.
6. Manual play, delegated play and quick simulation reconcile at the same boundary. No hidden reroll, duplicate reward, silently dropped choice or stale-tab overwrite can decide the career.
7. Old saves remain readable, including long careers and retirement/postcareer history. Storage errors explain recovery. Recorded cup campaigns and era snapshots stay intact within unchanged measured budgets.
8. Phone layouts keep the current action in view with usable tap targets, small tiles and a back button. A new panel does not become a long stacked page or make the page jump.
9. Meaningful remote outcome checks pass against the accepted baseline, with effective negative controls. Build, actual app types, applicable simulation regressions, download/save budgets and all15 rebuilt-site readers pass before release credit. The release lead owns fresh integrated-main and publication proof.

These criteria define completion. Event count is a content measure, not a completion gate. Hundreds of events are useful only if they remain varied, contextual, replayable and attached to these working loops.
