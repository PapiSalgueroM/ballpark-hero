# Football career benchmarks, 2026-10-13

The best fit for DoUKnowBall is a manager's working week: notice a problem, make a football decision, then see and remember its consequences. Our engines already support much of that work. Connecting those actions into a clear agenda is more useful than reproducing another game's menus or adding messages with no lasting effect.

This is a bounded research input, not an exhaustive feature inventory or a hands-on review. External evidence below comes only from publisher editorial, release and help pages. Community requests on publisher forums are not feature evidence. Platform editions differ. Mechanics are paraphrased; no product copy, artwork, licensed assets or interface is proposed for reuse. Rival names stay in docs only.

## Recommended order for a free browser game

1. **A small manager agenda derived from the actual next fixture.** Show recovery/training, selection and roles, one opponent brief, a pending contract or scout report, and any real deadline. Every item should open an existing action and disappear or change when that action is completed. Allow a quick route through routine work.
2. **Consequences that come back.** Track an existing playing-time promise, an academy graduate's first appearances, an injury return or a staff vacancy through real calendar advances. Explain why the follow-up appeared and what changed. Use generated people or role speakers; do not invent dialogue or misconduct by real players.
3. **Evidence-based advice.** Staff and scouts should explain available information, costs and uncertainty. Derive advice from actual engine state. A recommendation must not silently grant knowledge, money, development or injury recovery.
4. **Player-career continuity.** Tie preparation, agent costs, loans, injuries and ordinary off-pitch choices to existing attributes, finances and later opportunities. Prioritize distinct tradeoffs and reliable reload over a large volume of interchangeable cards.
5. **Preserve long careers before growing the world.** Hold save schemas, numeric caps and ordinary simulation gates. Diagnose the current size failure causally before adding more retained history. Defer licensed assets, 3D presentation, social hubs, live-data scenarios and a much larger league universe.

These priorities are design inferences from the sourced mechanics and the inspected seams, not measured engagement or parity claims. Strong remote proof would use actual legal actions and calendar advances, an independent effect oracle, unanswered/declined preservation, answer-once and expiry checks, reload of pending/resolved work, and source controls that remove delivery or neutralize an effect and must fail the specific assertions. Keep current floors, budgets and source holds unchanged.
