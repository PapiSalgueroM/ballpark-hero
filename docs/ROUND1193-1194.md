# Rounds 1193-1194: Soccer Career season history

Base: Release AR fa24b3848d99e29367486489b081a483dc544206.
Branch: codex/career-season-history. No dependency on the other READY drafts.

## Design contract

Objective: look back at two seasons side by side, and see when injuries or bans
affected the career, using only the facts already saved.

This extends Ratings inside Soccer Career. It does not add a route, score,
simulation choice, reward, daily puzzle or new stored field. Existing US career
comparison and queued Soccer Record Book do not provide this two-season view.

1193: choose two distinct saved senior rows. Original save indices identify them,
including duplicate years, loans and seasons with no appearances. The latest two
senior rows are the default. Show position-appropriate appearance and production
stats, the saved overall and played match rating. Change means second minus first.
Unknown values stay unknown, including old defender clean sheets. Do not substitute
today's overall, infer minutes or show any award result.

1194: show recorded injury names, weeks and severity, actual saved suspension
matches and saved reasons for seasons without appearances. Recovery weeks are
not matches missed. Missing legacy fields do not mean zero.

Mobile controls live inside one shared Ratings dialog. Label both selectors, use
44px buttons, contain any wide content, and provide a Back button. Reopenable Help
includes rules and a worked example. Returning restores the chosen seasons,
Ratings scroll and the originating control's focus. Closing restores page focus
and body scrolling.

Data: saved SeasonRecord fields and existing rating reader semantics. No real
sports data, quotes, external images or new database access. The engine, existing
readers, save format, scores, awards and random stream remain unchanged.
