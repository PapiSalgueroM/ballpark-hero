## Product contract

Objective: make yourself available for your country again after international retirement, then earn your place through the actual squad selector.

- Existing international retirement already exists. This adds an intentional availability return, distinct from the existing random comeback event. No new retirement action, historical cap adjustment or instant recall.
- A capped adult senior player aged18 to44 can choose it from the playing phase after pending summaries, tournaments, awards, rehab and event decisions finish. No bypass of a saved outcome.
- Reading, first/reopened rules, review, Back/Escape and reload do not mutate the career or draw randomness.
- Confirmation restores only internationalCareer and intStats.isRetired, preserving every existing captain/history/stat field. It adds one honest availability event and a strict optional receipt bound to actual recorded count/year/age/nationality/caps.
- One return per actual recorded season. Retirement again, training, transfers, same-year loan moves, stale page callbacks and reload cannot reset it. A later actual recorded season can permit a new return; no clock-derived cooldown.
- Original squad selection and qualification decide future appearances. Weak form, age penalties, bans, prison, injury and failed qualification can still yield no caps. No new rating, wages, money, morale, trophy, guaranteed place or coach quote.
- Capped returnees bypass only the first-ever debut activation condition by restoring the existing active-career flag. No change to first-call age limits or selectors, no duplicate debut age/year.
- Old and inactive careers retain full saved-state and RNG behavior. Malformed or future receipts fail closed for this action and never repair/drop unrelated save data. No migration field is added merely by reading.
- Compact card plus bounded dialog, first/reopenable rules and a worked example,44px new controls, exact opener focus/body/page return. Existing interface and utility docks retain their own dimensions and scrolling limitations.
- No external fact or player roster is added. Existing RealismB456 random7-cap comeback stays unchanged and receives no new-behavior claim.
