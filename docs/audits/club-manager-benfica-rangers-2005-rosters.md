# Benfica and Rangers 2005-06 Club Manager roster sources

Each partial roster contains one goalkeeper and ten outfielders from the club's opening match squad. The 2005-06 UEFA Champions League technical report supplies the broad roles. [Benfica on printed page 72](https://www.uefa.com/newsfiles/458764.pdf#page=72), [Rangers on printed page 80](https://www.uefa.com/newsfiles/458764.pdf#page=80)

## Benfica

All 11 selected players started against Vitória de Setúbal in the August 13, 2005 Supertaça. UEFA's contemporary season preview confirms that the Supertaça opened the 2005-06 season. The federation's lineup uses Moreira, Anderson and Beto. UEFA identifies the goalkeeper as José Moreira. Benfica identifies Anderson as Anderson Cleber Beraldo and Luisão as Anderson Luís da Silva. Beto is Gilberto Galdino dos Santos. [UEFA Moreira match report](https://www.uefa.com/news-media/news/01ad-0e6d01c70249-ef67e0df1444-1000--sporting-increase-benfica-woes/), [Benfica 2006-07 annual report](https://media.slbenfica.pt/-/media/BenficaDP/Images/Instituicao/SAD/prestacao-de-contas/anuais/23112007sportlisboaebenficafutebolsadinformarelatorioecontas20062007.pdf), [UEFA Benfica booking list](https://www.uefa.com/newsfiles/388579.pdf)

The season report lists Simão and Geovanni as forwards, and Benfica's May 14 lineup places them in the attacking line with Nuno Gomes. UEFA also describes Simão as a midfielder and records Geovanni playing in midfield against Lille. [Benfica May 14 lineup](https://www.slbenfica.pt/en-us/agora/noticias/2017_2018/10/13/benfica-futebol-luisao-derbi-golo-titulo), [UEFA Simão report](https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/news/025a-0eaa6f258be5-d94397400180-1000--miccoli-to-lead-benfica-line/), [UEFA September 14 Geovanni match report](https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/news/01ad-0ea8118ecf7e-500a9bfcee18-1000--miccoli-strike-sends-eagles-soaring/)

Georgios Karagounis and Fabrizio Miccoli arrived after August 13. Moretto, Marco Ferreira, Manduca, Laurent Robert and Marcel arrived in January. ## Rangers

The Scottish league archive and UEFA's contemporary match report date the opening win over Livingston to July 31, 2005. Rangers' archive labels the same 3-0 match July 30 and supplies the lineups. The league's date sets the cutoff. Rangers' opener lists Smith by surname, and the club's player profile identifies Steven Smith as a left-back. He covers the defense role in this pool. Fernando Ricksen started the opener in defense and also played midfield that season. UEFA's signing report confirms the spelling José Pierre-Fanfan, and its opener recap uses Dado Pršo. [Rangers Smith profile](https://www.rangers.co.uk/article/steven-smiths-academy-journey/1AbwfQnrZyZkmksNdxGD1O), [UEFA November Ricksen preview](https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/news/01af-0e6d189e282b-006fcacee469-1000--coaches-vow-to-push-for-points/), [UEFA Pierre-Fanfan signing](https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/news/025a-0ea9fc59fc91-8e34debd027d-1000--pierre-fanfan-ready-for-rangers/), [UEFA July 31 report](https://www.uefa.com/news-media/news/01ab-0e6ceae77772-c9b99954bdfb-1000--rangers-return-in-familiar-fashion/)

Ferguson returned from Blackburn Rovers on January 31, 2005 and captained Rangers in the opener. The opening bake puts him in the Rangers cup pool and drops his outdated Blackburn row. Historical saves can still hold his original player record and Scotland flag. [UEFA transfer report](https://www.uefa.com/news-media/news/01a6-0f8465b6372a-4398a884b3f0-1000--ferguson-returns-to-rangers/), [Blackburn club archive](https://www.rovers.co.uk/news/2019/july/club-connections/), [Rangers match squad](https://www.rangers.co.uk/article/rangers-3-0-livingston-4/3W0WovKztfjJ4dfuNmAk5P), [Scottish FA player record](https://www.scottishfa.co.uk/en/teams/mens-team/barry-ferguson)

Shota Arveladze signed for AZ in June. Francis Jeffers and Olivier Bernard arrived after the July opener, and Kris Boyd joined in January 2006.
