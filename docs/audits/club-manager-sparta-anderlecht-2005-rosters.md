# Sparta Prague and Anderlecht 2005-06 Club Manager roster sources

Both partial cup scorer pools contain the eleven players who started the named opening match. The player lines reflect the opening match and the season records. [Sparta Prague on printed page 99](https://www.uefa.com/newsfiles/458764.pdf#page=99), [Anderlecht on printed page 86](https://www.uefa.com/newsfiles/458764.pdf#page=86)

## Sparta Prague

All 11 selected players started the league opener at Viktoria Plzeň on August 6, 2005. The Czech league's match sheet and ČTK's same-day report agree on the lineup and date. The league's season roster confirms the full names and accents. [Chance Liga match sheet](https://www.chanceliga.cz/zapas/2878-plz-acs), [ČTK match report](https://www.sport.cz/clanek/fotbal-ceska-1-liga-fotbalisty-sparty-novacek-zplzne-nezaskocil-62382), [Chance Liga season roster](https://www.chanceliga.cz/klub/2006/kadr/2-ac-sparta-praha)

Chance Liga labels Sivok as a defender. ČTK's match report places him beside Martin Hašek in midfield at the opener, and UEFA's season report lists him in midfield. Poborský captained Sparta in the opener. The pool excludes Tomáš Poláček and Luboš Loučka, whom Sparta signed at the end of the August window. [ČTK match report](https://www.sport.cz/clanek/fotbal-ceska-1-liga-fotbalisty-sparty-novacek-zplzne-nezaskocil-62382), [UEFA technical report](https://www.uefa.com/newsfiles/458764.pdf#page=99), [UEFA Poláček and Loučka signings](https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/news/01ad-0ea7d44802b2-347bbffe0d3e-1000--last-gasp-signings-boost-sparta/)

## Anderlecht

All 11 selected players started the July 26, 2005 home win over Neftchi in the first leg of the second Champions League qualifying round. UEFA's match sheet supplies the starting XI. The DFB season archive and contemporary Belgian match reporting corroborate the squad. [UEFA fixture](https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/match/81465/), [UEFA match sheet data](https://match.uefa.com/v5/matches/81465/lineups?language=EN), [DFB season roster](https://datencenter.dfb.de/en/competitions/champions-league/seasons/2005-2006/teams/rsc-anderlecht), [Contemporary match report](https://www.anderlecht-online.be/nl/article/3191)

The UEFA technical report lists Deschacht in midfield and Vanderhaeghe in defense. The qualifier match data and the DFB archive classify Deschacht as a defender and Vanderhaeghe as a midfielder. Anderlecht's own lineup archive also places them in defense and midfield, respectively. [UEFA technical report](https://www.uefa.com/newsfiles/458764.pdf#page=86), [UEFA match sheet data](https://match.uefa.com/v5/matches/81465/lineups?language=EN), [DFB season roster](https://datencenter.dfb.de/en/competitions/champions-league/seasons/2005-2006/teams/rsc-anderlecht), [Anderlecht lineup archive](https://www.rsca.be/fr/preview-rwdand)

The Serbian association and UEFA's later transfer notice spell the striker's name Nenad Jestrović. The qualifier data uses the variant Ještrović. The Czech federation confirms Daniel Zítka, and Anderlecht confirms Pär Zetterberg. Mbo Mpenza is the elder brother of Emile Mpenza, who is already in the Hamburg roster. [Serbian FA profile](https://fss.rs/moja-reprezentacija-nenad-jestrovic/?script=lat), [UEFA Jestrović transfer notice](https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/news/01c4-0e6e6edc854c-726ec2847eab-1000--crvena-zvezda-bring-jestrovic-home/), [Czech FA Zítka profile](https://www.fotbal.cz/nasfotbal/muj-prvni-gol-daniel-zitka/a6935), [Anderlecht Hall of Fame](https://www.rsca.be/en/Pro_League_Hall_of_Fame), [UEFA Mpenza signing](https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/news/019e-0e6b4f58ef20-b72fe293c9e2-1000--anderlecht-look-to-mpenza/)

Roland Juhász and Grégory Pujol joined after the July 26 qualifier.
