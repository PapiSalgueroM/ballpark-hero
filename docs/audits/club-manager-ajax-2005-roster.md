# Ajax 2005-06 Club Manager roster sources

The partial roster represents verified Ajax first-team membership at the August 2005 season opening. The rows use broad positions. They document roster coverage across two sources per player. UEFA's end-of-season technical report gives broad positions and includes winter registrations. Opening match records and career dates establish who was already with Ajax in August. [UEFA 2005-06 technical report, PDF page 68][uefa-season]

## Cutoff and gaps

- UEFA and Ajax date Klaas-Jan Huntelaar's move to the winter of 2005-06. His February appearance against Inter belongs to the spring roster. [UEFA winter update][uefa-winter], [Ajax winter signing history][ajax-huntelaar], [Inter match archive][inter-february]
- UEFA reported a preseason shoulder injury that was expected to keep Stekelenburg out at the start. His membership is verified and Vonk's August appearance is verified separately. This source set covers a partial roster across four lines. It establishes neither a full August starting XI nor complete season registration. [UEFA preseason report][uefa-preseason], [UEFA second leg][uefa-second]
- UEFA and Brøndby agree on Ajax's scorers in both August matches. They differ on the minute of Sneijder's first goal in the return leg and on the attribution of Brøndby's late equalizer in the first leg. Neither disputed detail enters the roster. The rows contain only names and broad roles. [UEFA first leg][uefa-first], [UEFA second leg][uefa-second], [Brøndby club archive][brondby]
