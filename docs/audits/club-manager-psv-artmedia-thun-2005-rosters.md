# PSV, Artmedia Bratislava and Thun 2005-06 Club Manager roster sources

Each partial cup scorer pool contains the 11 starters from the named opening match. The lines represent verified broad player roles. [PSV on printed page 79](https://www.uefa.com/newsfiles/458764.pdf#page=79), [Artmedia Bratislava on printed page 87](https://www.uefa.com/newsfiles/458764.pdf#page=87), [Thun on printed page 100](https://www.uefa.com/newsfiles/458764.pdf#page=100)

## PSV

The same 11 started against Ajax in the August 5, 2005 Johan Cruijff Shield and at Heracles Almelo in the August 13 league opener. PSV's contemporary pre-match squad announcement confirms the lines. It groups Ibrahim Afellay and Theo Lucius with the midfielders. Afellay also played wide in the Shield. ## Artmedia Bratislava

All 11 selected players started away at Kairat Almaty on July 12, 2005, in Artmedia's first Champions League qualifier. UEFA's match data and the Slovak wire service TASR agree on the starting lineup. [UEFA fixture](https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/match/81176/), [UEFA match sheet data](https://match.uefa.com/v5/matches/81176/lineups?language=EN), [TASR campaign records](https://sportnet.sme.sk/spravy/artmedia-najvacsim-prekvapenim-rocnika-2005-06/)

Tchuř started in defense, although he played midfield in other matches that season. The technical report lists Staňo in midfield and Obžera as a forward. TASR includes Staňo in the opening back line and Obžera in the midfield line. The technical report lists Borbély in defense. TASR puts him in midfield at the opener and UEFA also describes him as a midfielder. [TASR campaign records](https://sportnet.sme.sk/spravy/artmedia-najvacsim-prekvapenim-rocnika-2005-06/), [UEFA technical report](https://www.uefa.com/newsfiles/458764.pdf#page=87), [UEFA Borbély transfer profile](https://www.uefa.com/news-media/news/01c9-0f84cb368b4e-84a673ce1e34-1000--floodgates-open-in-romania/)

Juraj Halenár appeared in the July 27 qualifier against Celtic. Peter Petráš joined in August. Ján Kozák, Balázs Borbély and Ján Ďurica were with Artmedia at the opener and departed during the January window. [UEFA Celtic match report](https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/news/01ab-0e6ce87beef6-38947c214697-1000--halenar-hat-trick-shocks-celtic/), [UEFA Petráš transfer notice](https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/news/01ac-0e6cefbc1b4c-f32c50556a55-1000--petra-follows-halenar-to-artmedia/), [UEFA winter moves](https://www.uefa.com/news-media/news/01b1-0f848427aab0-a2e74226d381-1000--bordeaux-bring-in-beto/)

## Thun

All 11 selected players started at Dynamo Kyiv on July 26, 2005, Thun's first Champions League qualifier. Dynamo's match archive independently confirms the XI and Thun's club history confirms the date. The UEFA season report gives most of the player lines. The qualifier data and DFB register identify David Pallas as a defender. [UEFA fixture](https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/match/81473/), [UEFA match sheet data](https://match.uefa.com/v5/matches/81473/lineups?language=EN), [Dynamo Kyiv lineup archive](https://fcdynamo.com/en/news/dinamo_vse_pomnit), [Thun club history](https://125.fcthun.ch/de/zur-geschichte/meilensteine), [DFB season roster](https://datencenter.dfb.de/competitions/champions-league/seasons/2005-2006/teams/fc-thun)

The qualifier data reverses the name order of Leandro Vieira and Adriano Pimenta. UEFA uses the names in the table in its contemporary reporting. Adriano Pimenta is a separate player from Inter's Adriano. Dynamo Kyiv and the Bosnian federation use the spelling Selver Hodžić. Thun uses Andres Gerber without an accent. [UEFA Thun match report](https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/news/01af-0e6d21064505-01ad95b9e53d-1000--last-gasp-ajax-cull-thun/), [Bosnian federation Hodžić profile](https://www.nfsbih.ba/en/news/football-w/a-national-team-w/selver-hodzic-my-heart-is-with-bih/), [Thun Gerber profile](https://125.fcthun.ch/de/zur-geschichte/portraets)

Pallas left for second-tier Bochum at the end of August, after starting the qualifier. Thun added Grégory Duruz that August, and Daniel Lopar appears in the wider roster from March 2006. Both arrived after the opening lineup.
