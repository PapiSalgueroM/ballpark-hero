# NBA, MLB, NHL and soccer ratings inventory

Date: 2026-10-02. Scope: Codex891 read-only source and committed-data audit, refreshed against the local Release V merge. NFL is assigned to the separate Round 889 lane. No production data, game logic, guides, saves or backend records were changed by this audit.

This report establishes what the code computes. It does not establish that every underlying sports fact is correct. The production database incident is still an operational boundary: no live site, Supabase, browser, remote API or external-source requests were made. Dates and source descriptions below are metadata carried by the existing files, not new independent verification.

## Concrete findings

1. **P1: current soccer has incompatible player-rating methods.** Club Manager uses the baked value-only curve. Squad Deal uses a different curve and boosts older players before applying it. Six matching named records produce different numbers, even when the same Club Manager value is converted to the original USD basis and fed into the actual Squad Deal function. This is a verified calculation inconsistency, not a claim that either resulting rating is the correct real-world number.
2. **P1: NHL ratings cannot currently be reproduced from the repository.** The 416 shipped rows contain name, position, age and OVR. The stated 2025-26 stat inputs and `bake_nhl.py` are absent. `docs/WORKBOARD.md:9273` independently records that the original bake scripts were not committed. A rating recalibration would require recovering the recipe and verified inputs first.
3. **P2: real-team career difficulty does not share the other games' team-strength baseline.** NBA, MLB and NHL My Career each roll opening team quality from 70 to 89 without using the assigned franchise's GM roster or Conquest strength. Later quality is a random walk clamped to 64 to 95. This is confirmed simulated behavior, not incorrect historical data. It matters if the new product requirement is recognizable franchise strength across games.
4. **P2: NBA Conquest retains two team-strength meanings.** Denver's seed has `rating: 92`, `offense: 92`, `defense: 78`, `overall: 85`. The power ranking displays the O/D average, while `generateFinalScore` receives the separate `rating` for its winner-score bonus. This hidden dual model is concrete; it does not prove Denver should have any particular real-world strength.
5. **Data blocker: NBA's named-player OVR and age inputs are not a reproducible season-stat model.** The actual GM initializer copies hand-entered OVR directly, then generates age from OVR buckets and RNG. Those generated ages affect contract length, potential and subsequent aging. Current talent and actual age cannot be certified from that method. A truthful statistical replacement needs verified player-season inputs and birth dates, not manually inflated familiar names.

## Inputs that can and cannot support a repair

**MLB is reproducible offline.** `scripts/genMlbFrontOfficeRoster.mjs`, `scripts/lib/mlbFoRecord.mjs`, `scripts/data/mlbRosters2026.json`, `scripts/data/mlbStats2026.json` and `scripts/data/mlbRostersLeftOut2026.json` provide a coherent input-to-seed chain. Metadata records official Stats API URL templates, retrieval dates and season. ESPN's recorded second-source check covered membership, birth date, bats and throws for 50 players on five clubs. It does not independently verify all season statistics.

Actual formula detail: qualified OPS midrank percentile maps to 66 + 31*pct. Samples below 300 PA multiply the percentile contribution by PA/300. Qualified starter FIP-lite percentile maps to 66 + 30*(1-pct), weighted by outs/300; relievers use 64 + 28*(1-pct), weighted by outs/120. A closer has 20+ saves and receives +2. Partial thresholds are fewer than 100 PA, 100 starter outs or 40 reliever outs. These are original game-model choices. The recipe is a one-season performance proxy, not a multi-year talent estimate and not a defensive-skill model.

Seven sampled shipped ratings exactly matched re-execution of the real `rateMan` over committed records: Gabriel Moreno 96, Zac Gallen 68, Gunnar Henderson 78, Shohei Ohtani 96, Aaron Judge 94, Corey Seager 82 and Vladimir Guerrero Jr. 74. This verifies derivation, not the truth of the cached inputs. No replacement ratings were invented.

**NBA needs verified current input rows.** Its committed `scripts/data/nbaHigherLowerVerified2026-09.json` contains career points, read 2026-09-22, not current-season role, efficiency or defensive inputs. Lifetime points are inadequate evidence for a current talent rating. The roster file's bio-table and editorial comments cannot substitute for a reproducible model or actual birth dates.

**NHL needs recipe recovery and inputs.** `scripts/data/nhlHigherLowerVerified2026-09.json` contains career points, read 2026-09-21. It has historical coverage limits and does not contain goalie rating inputs. It cannot recreate current P/GP, save percentage or wins-percentile ratings. Original seed rows also lack official player IDs and row-level basis/sample/confidence records.

**Soccer can support a bounded consistency repair from existing recorded inputs.** The Club Manager, club-depth-chart and Squad Deal recipes are present. They currently disagree. A shared current-player method could be evaluated without changing memberships, raw values, real statistics or historical squads. This would still be a model calibration over imperfect data, not verified ability. Historical eras and deliberately synthetic legends require separate treatment.

### Potential Round 892: bounded current-soccer consistency plan, not claimed

The concrete candidate is to reuse the existing original Squad Deal current-player method, not invent a third calibration or copy outside ratings. Put its exact USD-million, age and clamp calculation in a pure module shared by card games and current-roster adapters. Leave current card outputs byte-for-byte equivalent. On an unchanged current source row, Club Manager's new opening league, current market card and applicable current depth-chart display should use that same calculation. The six measured disagreements then have a deterministic acceptance target: 83, 86, 86, 92, 87 and 92 respectively, on the exact existing inputs. Those targets are model consistency, not new factual claims of ability.

This cannot honestly be completed through Squad Deal alone. Round 883's Liga MX bake is now merged; Claude retains Club Manager source ownership and Round 890 profiling. No Club Manager engine, roster generator, current bake, opponent-strength adapter, national/club depth-chart generator or guide may be touched until ownership is cleared and the exact scope is claimed. A pure helper extraction with unchanged callers would be preparation only; it would not fix the reported discrepancy.

Before a production cutover, measure a candidate over the unchanged bakes and include every opening strength reader: own squad, opponent roster, club preview, market, national/club chart where current data exists, and generated-player progression. Otherwise only the human club could gain a rating advantage while opponents stay on the old curve. The current generators omit some age fields in their chart output, so age-aware consistency must use an existing verified age input or explicitly keep that chart outside the claim. No age may be guessed from name, fame or career length.

Financial acceptance must be explicit:

- Preserve every stored real player's `value`, contract wage, budget and saved outcome. `wageFor` at `clubManager.ts:5566` prices an ordinary player's stored value; `sellValue` at `:7532` also prefers stored value. These branches can remain financially unchanged when the visible/current simulation rating changes.
- Do not replace `baseValue`, `rawCurveValue` or `bakedValueForRating` indiscriminately. They currently price padding, founders and projected prospects on a different curve, and historical era transforms depend on that interpretation. For any newly generated current-scale player that needs a synthetic price, the interior inverse of the chosen current curve is `USDmillions = 10^((rating-62)*log10(400)/33) / ageBoost(age)`, then the existing 0.75 conversion supplies model GBP millions. Clamped endpoints and rounded OVR are not uniquely invertible. This must be labeled a simulated price and verified with financial baselines, never written over recorded real market value.
- New current saves require a rating-method version so later loads know which scale their squad, opponents and generated prospects use. Preserve existing unversioned save bytes and behavior. Recomputing ratings during every load would destroy earned progression; applying the new world to an old save without a deliberate compatibility branch could change opponent difficulty mid-career.
- Historical eras, peak legends and progressed future players remain separate. A 2010 roster, a peak-era card and a current opening player should not be equated merely because the name matches.

Useful offline proof: exact old-to-new six-record consistency; complete current-card equivalence; current bake/fallback equality using unchanged row values and age; unknown-age/value confidence handling; own/opponent parity; new-save/reload equivalence; untouched old-save outcomes; budgets, wage bills, renewal asks, trade/sale returns, generated padding/founder value and multi-season money baselines. Controls must restore the actual old curve, remove the age boost, cross USD/GBP units, or rerate an old progressed save and show the corresponding independent outcome fails. A calibration-only script must not be presented as a production repair or a verified roster refresh.
