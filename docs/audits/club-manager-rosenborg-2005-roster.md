# Rosenborg 2005-06 Club Manager roster sources

This partial roster covers 11 Rosenborg players who had appeared by the August 2005 opening. Every player appeared against Bodø/Glimt on July 31 and against Steaua București on August 10. Frode Johnsen came on in July, and Fredrik Winsnes came on in August. Each broad role appears in UEFA's 2005-06 technical report and matches the role in Rosenborg's own player archive. ## Cutoff

Alejandro Lago made his Rosenborg debut on July 24, before the opening cutoff. Steffen Iversen returned on February 13, 2006, so his move belongs to the later squad. The data records names and broad roles from the opening pool.
