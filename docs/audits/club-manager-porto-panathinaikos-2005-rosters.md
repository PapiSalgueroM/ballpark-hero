# Porto and Panathinaikos 2005-06 Club Manager roster sources

These partial cup scorer pools contain the 11 starters from each club's named August opener. The roles are broad and reflect contemporary match reporting and season records. [Porto on printed page 95](https://www.uefa.com/newsfiles/458764.pdf#page=95), [Panathinaikos on printed page 94](https://www.uefa.com/newsfiles/458764.pdf#page=94)

## Porto

All 11 players started the August 21, 2005 league opener against Estrela da Amadora. The contemporary Diário de Notícias report gives the lineup and the league's footage confirms the date and match. The DFB season register corroborates all 11 players. [Diário de Notícias match report](https://www.dn.pt/arquivo/diario-de-noticias/dragoes-espantaram-fantasma-caseiro.html), [Liga Portugal opener footage](https://ligaportugal.vsports.pt/vod/4090/m/921/forza/04d74f4977150ddf8f5d59ae33d80263), [DFB season register](https://datencenter.dfb.de/competitions/19/seasons/2005-2006/teams/686)

César Peixoto played left-back in the opener, while UEFA's season report lists him among the forwards. The opener report places Jorginho and Lisandro on the wings around striker Hélder Postiga. The DFB lists Postiga as a forward. The Portuguese federation gives Meireles's full name as Raul José Trindade Meireles, while UEFA and the DFB use Raúl. Diego is Diego Ribas da Cunha. Marek Čech joined on August 31. Adriano Vieira Lousada joined in January 2006. Both arrived after the cutoff. ## Panathinaikos

All 11 players started away at Wisła Kraków on August 9, 2005, in the first leg of the third Champions League qualifying round. UEFA's match data and Wisła's club archive confirm the lineup. The Polish federation archive independently records the match. [UEFA fixture](https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/match/81612/), [UEFA match sheet data](https://match.uefa.com/v5/matches/81612/lineups?language=EN), [Wisła lineup archive](https://archiwum.wisla.krakow.pl/pl/aktualnosci/byl_taki_mecz_krakowska_radosc_grecka_tragedia/), [Polish federation match archive](https://www.laczynaspilka.pl/biblioteka/mecze/wisla-krakow-panathinaikos-ateny-31-09082005)

Wisła groups Šerić with the midfielders in its opener lineup. UEFA's contemporary preview identifies him as a left-back, and the technical report lists him as a defender. The qualifier data labels Bišćan a defender. Wisła's opening lineup, UEFA's technical report and Panathinaikos's own archive place him in midfield. UEFA's API uses Fanis Gekas and Dimitris Papadopoulos, while its match and season reports use Theofanis and Dimitrios. [UEFA opening preview](https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/news/0254-0d7b541b57f3-f08104d432e2-1000--panathinaikos-primed-for-wisla-test/), [UEFA technical report](https://www.uefa.com/newsfiles/458764.pdf#page=94), [Panathinaikos player archive](https://www.pao.gr/en/historyitem/all-time-foreigners/), [UEFA Greece squad](https://www.uefa.com/uefaeuro/history/news/0254-0d7b9a7c9ab6-78210d00455e-1000--samaras-set-for-greece-duty/)

Bišćan joined Panathinaikos from Liverpool on a free transfer announced July 16. The former Liverpool opening row used the exact display name `Igor Biscan`. New cup scorer entries use the club's spelling, Igor Bišćan. The scorer pool checks the older spelling when determining whether an existing save still assigns him elsewhere. [UEFA July 16 transfer report](https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/news/0254-0d7b8a7f286d-2f65117dd96f-1000--panathinaikos-plump-for-midfield-pair/), [Liverpool player profile](https://www.liverpoolfc.com/igor-biscan), [Panathinaikos player archive](https://www.pao.gr/en/historyitem/all-time-foreigners/)

The Sándor Torghelle loan was announced on August 9. He missed the registration deadline for the Wisła tie.
