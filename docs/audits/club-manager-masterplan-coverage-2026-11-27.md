# Club Manager coverage against all 60 masterplan cards

November 27, 2026. The source links pin the Club Manager code used for the published corner release, entry `index-Cdnjm2PD.js`. The card numbers follow the manager chapter of the expansion masterplan. This inventory covers Club Manager's own career and connected match screens.

**Implemented slice** means the named decision has a working player action and consequence. **Partial** means some relevant mechanics exist and a central part of the card still needs evidence or development. **Unverified** means the core player decision was not established by this review. The statuses describe the stated scope. The audit did not replay every historical rule, interaction or career length.

### Where the next decisions would change play

1. **Press a specific return pass.** In 40 ordinary league fixtures there were 610 opposing throws, 589 with a named thrower. At 120 of the 585 distinct opposing throw times the engine had already scheduled a shot. Record an eligible return pass, a regain or a bypass and the cost to the presser. Validate the two outcomes on the same fixture and keep independent shots separate.
2. **Follow a senior transfer target over time.** Reuse the existing brief, shortlist and scout desk. Make a manager choose which of two candidates receives a second viewing before deadline day. Preserve the first report, show what the second observation changed and disclose when the game lacks playing-time data.
3. **Make a future transfer payment genuinely conditional.** Reuse the existing fee negotiator and accounts. Let an appearance milestone compete with cash up front, then show the exact trigger and its projected cash impact before acceptance. Verify the obligation after a job move and a season rollover before extending the clause list.
4. **Give one more second tier its actual playoff path.** The game already has a calendar and tie resolution. Pick a verified competition and era, then test every qualifying table position through the draw, both clubs' finances and their next-season division. The current rule descriptions explicitly name the Spanish and Italian simplifications.
