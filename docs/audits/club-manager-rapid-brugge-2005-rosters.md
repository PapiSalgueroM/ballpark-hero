# Rapid Vienna and Club Brugge 2005-06 Club Manager roster sources

Each partial cup scorer pool has one goalkeeper and ten outfielders. The UEFA Champions League technical report supplies the broad roles, with the qualifier match sheet supplying Michael Klukowski's role. [Rapid Vienna on printed page 96](https://www.uefa.com/newsfiles/458764.pdf#page=96), [Club Brugge on printed page 89](https://www.uefa.com/newsfiles/458764.pdf#page=89), [UEFA qualifier match sheet data](https://match.uefa.com/v5/matches/81600/lineups?language=EN)

## Rapid Vienna

All 11 selected players started against Sturm Graz in the July 13, 2005 Austrian Bundesliga opener. The Austrian league match sheet and UEFA report agree on every selected player's broad role. The German federation's season roster confirms, among others, the spellings Jozef Valachovič and Axel Lawarée. The Hungarian association spells the other accented name György Korsós, using Hungarian surname-first order. ## Club Brugge

All 11 selected players were in the August 9, 2005 squad at Vålerenga, Club Brugge's opening Champions League qualifier. Ten started and Manasseh Ishiaku came on as a substitute. Contemporary Norwegian match coverage corroborates those appearances. Contemporary Belgian reporting also names seven of the 11 in the August 5 league opener against Mouscron. The UEFA qualifier sheet establishes the cutoff for the full pool. [UEFA fixture](https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/match/81600--valerenga-vs-club-brugge/), [UEFA qualifier match sheet data](https://match.uefa.com/v5/matches/81600/lineups?language=EN), [VG August 9 match report](https://www.vg.no/sport/i/zL5gnK/tirsdagens-champions-league-kvalifisering), [Voetbalkrant August 5 match report](https://www.voetbalkrant.com/belgie/jupiler-pro-league/2005-2006/kalender/speeldag-1/club-brugge/moeskroen)

The UEFA technical report lists Clement as a defender, while the qualifier report places him in midfield that night. The season report classifies Verheyen as a forward. UEFA's qualifier data and January signing report identify Klukowski as a defender, and Canada Soccer records his 2005 Belgian Super Cup win with Brugge. Qualifier match data and the DFB player profile confirm the spelling Manasseh Ishiaku. [UEFA technical report](https://www.uefa.com/newsfiles/458764.pdf#page=89), [UEFA Klukowski and Ishiaku signing report](https://www.uefa.com/news-media/news/01a5-0f8462b22d1c-46a8d454d939-1000--trochowski-takes-hamburg-option/), [Canada Soccer Klukowski profile](https://canadasoccer.com/profile/1742/mike-klukowski/?camp=221), [DFB Ishiaku profile](https://datencenter.dfb.de/en/data-center/people/manasseh-ishiaku/player)

DFB's September 27 Bayern match sheet corroborates Butina, De Cock, Maertens, Englebert, Vermant, Leko, Verheyen and Balaban. Club Brugge's own archives separately confirm Clement and Ishiaku's membership. [DFB match sheet](https://datencenter.dfb.de/datencenter/champions-league/2005-2006/2/bayern-muenchen-fc-bruegge-646386), [Club Brugge internationals archive](https://www.clubbrugge.be/nl/news/hoe-doen-onze-internationals-het), [Club Brugge history](https://olympia.clubbrugge.be/en/timeline/2000)

Leko left Málaga for Hajduk Split in January 2005 and signed for Club Brugge on June 20. He played in the July 30 Super Cup. The 2005-06 pool includes Leko. It excludes Javier Portillo, whose loan from Real Madrid was announced on August 31.
