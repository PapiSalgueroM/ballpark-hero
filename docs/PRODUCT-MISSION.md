# DoUKnowBall Product Mission

Received from Anthony 2026-08-29, verbatim in substance, the companion to the
Master Build Spec and the Owner Directives. The one operational addition both
lanes build to: WHERE A GAME SUITS IT, four ways to play: Solo, Versus CPU,
Versus Anyone (public matchmaking), Versus Friends (private room code), and a
human queue that cannot fill quickly hands the player a CPU opponent instead
of a dead lobby. Search and Discard and Sports Bingo already carry the solo,
CPU and pass-and-play thirds; online rooms remain the spec P3 arc.

The mission: the best free sports gaming playground on the internet, for
people who want to test sports knowledge AND people who simply want to play.
No console, no seventy dollar title, no subscription, no group of friends
required. The fantasies the platform grows toward: become an athlete, manage
a team, build a dynasty, trade, draft, compete with real people, play actual
interactive sports, build a life and career, arcade games, knowledge games,
stories worth sharing. Never a literal clone of any commercial title:
original DoUKnowBall games that fulfill the same desires with their own
mechanics, artwork, identity and systems (the standing rival names and
legal fences enforce the boundary mechanically).

The philosophy, word for word his: free to enter, easy to understand, fun
immediately, deep enough to play for hours, playable alone, better with
friends, competitive with strangers, accessible from an ordinary browser.
Sixty seconds or six hours, both natural. The site someone thinks of when
they say: I am bored, I want to play something.
