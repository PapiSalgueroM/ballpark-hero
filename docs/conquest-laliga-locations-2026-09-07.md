# Soccer Conquest: La Liga ground locations

Verified 2026-09-07 for the existing ESP club slice. Research preparation only. This does not claim that a geographic La Liga preset is implemented. No source code, membership, roster, strength or database changes.

## Result and scope

19 clubs have supported current ground identities and stadium-scale candidate anchors. RAY is withheld because its current home venue is unsettled: the latest completed home match was at Butarque, while the club is seeking to return to Vallecas. Do not substitute a borrowed match venue as its permanent home or silently use its historic home as a current venue. This is a gate for a future current-ground preset, not for the England implementation.

Coordinates below are latitude, longitude in decimal degrees, rounded from the first linked coordinate source to four decimal places. They are suitable for a national territory map, not surveyed pitch centers, navigation or real club territory. The largest difference between the published, unrounded coordinate pairs is 80.3 metres (ATH). Treat spatial accuracy as approximately 100 metres, not the decimal precision shown.

Verification followed the data guardian skill: an official club or league source establishes ground identity and locality; an independent venue reference corroborates the geographic location. A further coordinate publisher is compared numerically. Coordinate-source lineage is stated below rather than claiming that every published number was independently surveyed.

## Venue changes, conflicts and implementation limits

- **RAY, unresolved current home:** the [league profile](https://www.laliga.com/clubes/rayo-vallecano) still labels the ground Estadio de Vallecas. The club's [2 September 2026 statement](https://www.rayovallecano.es/noticias/comunicado-oficial-estadio-2-septiembre) asks for the suspension of its stadium concession to be lifted. Its [3 September statement](https://www.rayovallecano.es/noticias/comunicado-oficial-rayo-real-racing-santander) prepares Butarque as the alternative for matchday 4. The [completed Rayo vs Racing match record](https://www.rayovallecano.es/noticias/rayo-vallecano-3-real-racing-club-2) identifies Estadio Ontime Butarque. The [current fixture page](https://www.rayovallecano.es/partidos) agrees. None establishes a settled season-long replacement. The Wikipedia account's proposed Coliseum venue and the ticket vendor's Vallecas fixture listings are superseded for that completed match by the official record. Keep RAY unresolved until current official evidence establishes the intended anchor, or explicitly define a historic-home convention in the future product.
- **BET, temporary ground:** the [club venue page](https://www.realbetisbalompie.es/club/la-cartuja) says La Cartuja is the temporary home during the Benito Villamarín rebuild. The [2026/27 season-ticket renewal announcement](https://www.realbetisbalompie.es/noticias/actualidad/55-474-socios-renuevan-su-abono-del-real-betis-balompie-para-la-temporada-2026-27-32524) confirms the current season at La Cartuja. Do not attach the current-ground anchor to the existing hex label Heliópolis. The Wikipedia page named only La Cartuja is the island/district, not the stadium; the specific stadium page is used.
- **BAR, return to Camp Nou:** the club's [current first-team schedule](https://www.fcbarcelona.com/en/football/first-team/schedule) lists September 2026 home matches at Spotify Camp Nou. Its [stadium page](https://www.fcbarcelona.com/en/club/instalaciones/spotify-camp-nou) independently supplies the club's venue identity. Do not reuse the former temporary Lluís Companys Olympic Stadium anchor. This verifies current scheduled venue use, not completion of every redevelopment phase.
- **VAL, no premature move:** the [current league profile](https://www.laliga.com/clubes/valencia-cf) names Mestalla. The club's [28 January 2026 construction update](https://www.valenciacf.com/the-construction-of-the-nou-mestalla-is-moving-forward-with-the-installation-of-the-first-pillars-stair-towers-and-roof-elements) plans the new ground for summer 2027. Nou Mestalla is not the current anchor in this record.
- **ATH, adjacent old and new venues:** the current [official stadium site](https://sanmames.athletic-club.eus/) and Wikipedia's explicitly 2013 stadium page identify the new San Mamés. The league profile's generic paragraph says 1913, which belongs to the previous ground and is not used to date the current one. The 80.3 metre Wikipedia/LatLong difference is retained transparently as stadium-scale uncertainty.

## Source lineage and release gate

Wikipedia is an independent venue publication relative to club and league websites, not an official source and not a surveyed geographic authority. Its coordinates can originate in Wikidata or other language editions. Those mirrors are not counted as extra independent confirmations.

Mapcarta exposes the OSM object or GeoNames record noted in the comparison table, often combined with Wikidata and Wikipedia descriptions. The numeric records offer useful location corroboration, but Mapcarta and Wikipedia prose are not independent sports-history sources. No direct OSM geometry was downloaded in this research pass, and this report does not assert an audited OSM editing lineage. LatLong explicitly disclaims guaranteed coordinate accuracy. TFC Stadiums and Football Ticket Club do not disclose the provenance of their coordinate numbers. The latter is used only for Vallecas geometry because its current-home claim conflicts with newer official match evidence.

The supported statement is club/ground identity and stadium-scale location, not survey-grade coordinates or guaranteed source independence for every underlying numerical observation. Further verification is required if the future implementation needs exact pitch centers, audited geographic provenance, or a claim about the stadium used on a specific match date.

Before activating a current-ground La Liga preset: resolve RAY without silently choosing a historic or one-match venue, retain BET's temporary-home label, recheck BAR/BET/VAL construction-related venue status, and preserve source URLs and this verification date beside any data copied into the application. This research does not authorize adding imagery, crests, rosters, ratings or competition results.
