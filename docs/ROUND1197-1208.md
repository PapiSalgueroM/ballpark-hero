## Gameplay contract

Objective: shape the next season through football decisions, then see the
recorded consequences. Optional planning preserves the existing one press loop.

1. Tactical roles: trade finishing, creation and defensive cover.
2. Secondary position: earn a compatible position through multiple played years.
3. Set pieces: negotiate duties and keep the resulting goal credit truthful.
4. Appearance promises: agree a target and settle it against recorded games.
5. Counteroffers: negotiate actual contract terms with an acceptance verdict.
6. Performance bonuses: pay only earned recorded appearance or performance money.
7. New club adaptation: choose a settling plan with selection and mood tradeoffs.
8. Loan to buy: make a real permanent move at the loan return boundary.
9. Managed return: choose availability versus injury risk after a serious injury.
10. Captain duties: use leadership decisions that affect play and discipline.

Choices belong to a club and an unplayed season. Changing club invalidates
club-specific plans. Settlements are once-only and name the actual year and
result. Two-year retraining cannot complete through repeated button presses.
No choice guarantees a trophy or reveals an award vote.

The four existing US My Careers then receive sport-specific planning for
workload, playing role, expectations, partnership, bonuses and late career
reinvention. Each uses its existing sport engine, positions and saved line.
Temporary engine inputs must be restored before long term player progression.

## Interface and data

Compact tiles, one detail at a time, Back, 44px controls and internal dialog
scroll. Rules and a worked example appear before the first decision. Help can
be reopened. Closing restores focus and the page position. Gameplay belongs
in optional saved fields; malformed fields safely give no effect.

No new real historical rosters, fixtures, stats, quotations, images or external services. New future cup opening ties are simulated from the existing checked association pool and saved once; historical missing matches stay missing. Chance wheels retain the actual original draw and cannot reroll a reward.
Future simulated choices and outcomes are labelled as game results. Existing
national competitions, honours, results and score models remain authoritative.
