# Anthony's full site review, 2026-08-28

Transcribed in full from his message the same day. This extends the 2026-08-26 tweaks
document and outranks the roadmap the same way. Work bugs first, in his order. Items marked
P1 are things he hit as broken during the review. He attached three screenshots: an
imperialism style conquest video (map format reference), the double footer, and a phone
video frame of the boot flash. He also owes screenshots of the FotMob quick sim layout he

None of these names may reach src, public or scripts.

## P1 bugs, fix before features

1. **The ticker shows nothing.** Games are on today and the strip is empty. Diagnose the
   whole chain: scores-poll edge function, API-Sports quota, the live_scores table, the
   frontend read. What he wants when it works: the ESPN cable model. Before a game: the
   matchup and start time, that day's full slate. During: the live score. After: FINAL and
   the score. He suggests loading the full season schedule ahead of time so the strip is
   never empty.
2. **Club Manager league tables are broken mid-season** (2005/06 era, Barcelona save,
   3 matches simmed). La Liga's own table is correct. Every OTHER league still says
   "pre-season alphabetical order" and shows no points and no games even after a transfer
   window passed. La Liga also appears TWICE in the picker, the starred one correct, the
   unstarred duplicate all zeros.
3. **The Cups tab is wrong.** It lists Copa del Rey as alive but renders the Champions
   League table under it. The projected quarter finals exclude his team while the group
   table shows him second. Wanted instead: the group table, and from the knockout weeks a
   full bracket that updates as rounds are played.
4. **Champions League group count**: the era shows 4 groups; 2005/06 really had 8 groups
   of 4. Verify what the engine simulates vs what the page shows. Related: 2010s eras must
   send third place to the Europa League knockouts; the 2026 era must use the real single
   table Swiss format. Style all tables and brackets on the FotMob look (colors, zone
   stripes, layout), for every competition, not just the UCL.
5. **Double footer** (screenshot): two full footers stacked on at least one page. Find
   which layouts double-mount Footer and fence it so it cannot return.
6. **Boot flash**: on first load a black screen of raw text shows for a split second (the
   prerender snapshot before React mounts). Style the snapshot pass so the handoff is
   invisible, without breaking what the snapshot exists for (crawlers).
7. **Who Am I data**: Rodri listed with age 0 and value 0. Sweep the pool for zero or null
   ages and values, fix the source, fence it.
8. **Squad Deal league filter**: picking La Liga dealt Premier League players. Also no
   flags anywhere in the game.
9. **Build Your XI position validity is stale**: Marcelo accepted at LB, ter Stegen
   accepted at CM. Position eligibility must come from current-ish data and a keeper can
   never be an outfielder.
10. **Sign the Player pricing**: Mile Svilar priced at 162m. Values must track market
    reality before the game is playable straight.
11. **Soccer Career social gram tells a goalkeeper to score more goals.** Position aware
    prompts.
12. **Trade Finder (US sports)**: only offensive players appear, and rosters are outdated.
13. **The Billion Dollar Game prices at 890m euros while being called the billion dollar
    game.** Price in dollars at one billion. Its points are also far too generous relative
    to other games.

## Decisions Anthony made in this review

- **Delete Overrated or Underrated.** Two buttons, no game feel. Retire the route with the
  standing retired-route pattern.
- **Delete Tier List.** Same reasoning, and only one list ever playable.
- **Leaderboard names**: he wants Kahoot style random names by default, is fine with real
  display names, but worries offensive names hurt the ad review. Decision taken on his
  "whatever u think": random sports word handles stay the default for everyone (legacy
  Baller-NNNN handles get regenerated to the word pool), custom display names stay allowed
  but pass a profanity blocklist before they ever render on a shared surface. Unmoderated
  user text on a public page is a genuine AdSense risk, so the filter is on.
- **Home hero headline is too long.** Replace with something like "100+ free games across
  every sport". The legal disclaimer stays in the footer where it lives; the hero does not
  need to carry it.
- **World Cup Bracket**: tournament is over; he is unsure it should stay. Repurpose the
  engine for the next live bracket or retire the route; do not leave a dead daily up.

## Club Manager, the big arc list

In his order, roughly. The P1 table bugs above come first.

- **Leagues and teams**: the full FIFA league list he sent earlier, plus snubbed leagues,
  in the modern game AND every era. More eras beyond the four when data can be verified.
  (Data rules hold: verifiable rosters or CM_PARTIAL, never invented players.)
- **Manager creation**: more nationalities, more preferred football styles. Real managers
  of all time as pickable with era spanning attributes, OR build your own with visual
  customization and a small animated face. LEGAL LIMIT: no real person's likeness. Real
  manager names with factual records are fine; animated faces are for generated people
  only. Design around that.
- **World editing**: move any club to any league before starting (super league style),
  with board expectations re-derived for the new league. God mode roster editor: free
  transfers between any clubs before kickoff.
- **Start options**: display currency, international job offers on or off, negotiation
  strictness slider.
- **Two meters, always visible**: board patience (how close to fired) and fan mood.
- **Staff**: hire and fire attack, defense, goalkeeping coaches, lead scout, and promote
  from the academy staff. Generated people with generated portrait art, each with levels
  and potential. Rivals can poach them; you can match offers a limited number of times.
- **Quick sim screen**: stoppage time shown the FotMob way (screenshots coming),
  possession as percentages, real team names never "them", center every text block,
  momentum graph reads as up and down swings.
- **Watch Live**: proper animation. Ball at players' feet, both teams with names and
  numbers on their dots, players cover the whole pitch, throw ins, corners and fouls
  exist. Live stats visible during play, subs and tactics at any moment, the AI opponent
  also subs. MERGE Play Match and Watch Live into one flow: manage the match live, or
  quick sim it.
- **Board asks get specific**: nationality quotas (usually the club's own country),
  experience counts, position targets, a 90+ potential signing, a 100m+ marquee buy scaled
  to era.
- **The messages inbox needs a major update**: more kinds of messages, choices that
  actually move relationships and futures.
- **Calendar**: click any day and sim to it (keep the four fast forwards), bigger emojis,
  match days name the opponent, transfer window open and close clearly marked.
- **Table**: show goals for and against as 25-23 alongside GD.
- **Finances**: ticket and concession pricing with fan and board reactions, sponsor offers
  (good and bad brands, local or global, negotiable), a full projected finances screen:
  player wages, staff wages, travel, transfer income, everything a club spends and earns.
- **Facilities**: dressing room, stadium, training ground, medical, each level 1 to 10,
  big clubs start high, small clubs start near zero, upgrades cost real money and help the
  squad.
- **Manager XP**: accomplishments earn skill points spent on a web style skill tree,
  each tier pricier, each reward bigger. Manager wages can also be invested in yourself.
- **Media**: pre and post match interviews stay, plus league wide articles and what the
  press writes about you, the 2K model.
- **Player and staff conversations**: trade requests, wage and game time asks, captaincy
  talks, and ordinary human conversations. (Standing legal rule: no invented quotes in a
  real person's mouth; write around it as the repo already does.)
- **Academy and scouting**: see potential, target positions, region filters, and show the
  academy squad we already have.
- **Squad page**: FIFA style spacing, age and stats in the row, hover card with form,
  wage happiness, game time happiness.
- **Tactics**: subs and reserves listed under the pitch, tap one player then another to
  swap. Out of position penalties, position retraining over weeks, but full freedom to
  place anyone anywhere (ten defenders if you want). Captain, corner takers left and
  right, free kick and penalty takers. Way more formations and variants. Player roles:
  attacking or holding fullbacks, sweeper keeper, and so on. Sub suggestions ordered by
  same position first.
- **Transfers, the big rework**: a valuation staffer whose accuracy depends on level
  (a level zero might say 120-160m for a 60m player); YOU type the bid; extreme lowballs
  can end talks entirely; sell on clauses, player swaps, a closeness meter, limited
  patience per negotiation. Loans with option and release figures. Then personal terms:
  length, wages, add ons, role promises, everything. FIFA's whole kit and more.
- **News choices**: "what would you do" situational articles, red card appeals, every
  real manager decision.
- **Multiplayer, future flag**: private rooms by code, humans take clubs in one world,
  room leader controls simming and restrictions. Big backend lift; design doc first.

  product names never in shipped files. The footer disclaimer stays.
