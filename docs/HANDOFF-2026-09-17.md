## 3. Round 619: free agents and contract termination

The request, verbatim from the footer form: *"Add free agents to Manager Mode, and allow players
to have their contracts terminated so they become free agents."* The same report praised the
recent second legs live start points, which is the first unprompted positive signal on that
change and is recorded in `docs/PROJECT-STATE.md`.

**What was already there, and why it was not this.** Since Round 105 a deal ticked down and a man
you never sat down with walked in the summer. The rollover called those players `freeAgentNews`,
said they left "for nothing", and then dropped them on the floor: out of the squad, and out of the
market too, because `buildMarket` reads the projected club rosters and a man who walked out on you
is in nobody's roster. The one thing the phrase promised, that somebody could sign him, had never
been true.

**Files.** `src/lib/clubManagerFreeAgents.ts` (new, the record, the payoff curve, the release
table, the demand decay, the world gate, `signedValue`, `uniqueFreeAgentId`), plus
`clubManager.ts`, `clubManagerFinances.ts`, `TransferScreen`, `ContractsCard`, `useClubManager`,
help and SEO copy, the regenerated search index, and `scripts/simClubManagerFreeAgents.mjs`.

**The rule that makes it worth having.** A free agent has no club, so no registration is being
traded between clubs, so the transfer window does not apply. `signFreeAgent` is the ONLY signing
path in the engine with no `transferWindow` check. Every other path stays locked and the harness
checks that too.

**The honesty gate.** The world only releases REAL players once the save has moved on
(`WORLD_POOL_FROM_YEAR`). Season one's world board is made up players, marked MADE UP as
everywhere else, because saying a real professional is unemployed while the world still IS the
real season reads as a claim about the man rather than about the save. Anyone you pay off yourself
joins under his own name that day, because that one is your doing.

**The review found a money printer.** `signFreeAgent` charged the signing on fee (18 percent of
notional value) and wrote the FULL notional value onto the squad record, while resale is 90
percent of value. **Everton turned 43m into 358.81m in one season.** Manchester City made 241m.
**Nine harness sections were green throughout, because all nine measured squad RATING and none
measured MONEY.** Fixed with `signedValue()`, anchored to the fee, so churning the board now loses
money at every club.

31 findings, 25 refuted, 6 confirmed, all 6 fixed. The other five: aged twice at the rollover
(30 of 30 walked players two years too old), retired twice (7 of 411), pool ids not unique
(Ederson and Ederson collide, signing one deletes both), `netSpend` blind to a payoff (Round 507's
mistake repeated), and the wage ceiling rentable with a loan out (bill 304 against a ceiling of
280, closed with `committedWageBill`).

## 4. Round 628: the season score reads the manager, not the club

The old rule was `Math.min(130, myRow.pts + seasonTrophies.length * 10)`, at
`src/lib/clubManager.ts` in both `currentSeasonScore` and the rollover. `docs/PROJECT-STATE.md`
listed "A correct points system per game" as PART with **Club Manager points design open**.

### The three defects, all measured before a line was written

**One, it read the club.** One season at twelve clubs with management held IDENTICAL (nobody
touched anything, every entry auto played), so every point of spread is stature and not
management. Scores ran 24 to 90 and the correlation between a club's preview XI rating and its
season score was **0.851**.

| club | board grade | objectives hit | score |
|---|---|---|---|
| Real Madrid | C | 1/8 | **90** |
| Newcastle | A, won the league | 3/6 | 78 |
| Bayern Munich | **F** | **0/7** | 55 |
| Sevilla | C | 3/8 | 46 |

**Two, the 130 scale did not fit the leagues.** Maximum league points by league size: 54 in the 10
club SuperSport HNL, 102 in the 18 club leagues, 114 in the big three, **138** in the 24 club EFL
Championship. A perfect Croatian season topped out near 84 with a cup treble and could never reach
the ceiling; the Championship passed 130 on league points alone so its trophies were worth nothing.

**Three, relegation paid.** Left in place eight seasons with nobody managing, Sunderland scored 43
finishing 18th in the Premier League, went down, won the Championship and scored **124**. Everton
reproduced it independently. Winning the second division beat winning the first by 46 points,
straight out of 46 games against 38.

### What it is now

A ledger in the new `src/lib/clubManagerScore.ts`, which is PURE and imports nothing from
`clubManager.ts`, so a harness can drive it with synthetic inputs and no engine bundle at all.
`clubManager.ts` owns the adapter (`seasonLedgerInputOf`).

| term | worth | reads |
|---|---|---|
| League form | up to 48 | your points over the points your own fixture list offered |
| The title | 28 | scaled by the share of the season you managed |
| A domestic cup run | up to 24 | `CUP_POINTS` indexed by `cupProgressRank`, 0 to 4 |
| A European run | up to 24 | `EURO_POINTS` indexed by `uclProgressRank`, 0 to 4 |
| Board objectives | 6 each, 30 max | **only at the final whistle**, see the trap below |

**48 + 28 + 24 + 30 is exactly 130**, which is the ceiling with NO European run in it. Five
playable leagues have no Champions League route at all (the Championship, the Saudi Pro League,
both MLS conferences, 2. Bundesliga, because `uclPlacesIn` returns 0 outside Europe), so pricing
Europe into the ceiling would have put the top of the scale permanently out of their reach, which
is the same bug this round exists to fix, one level up. A European league gets Europe's 24 as
slack instead. Two routes to the top, neither blocked by the league you picked.
`ceilingWithoutEurope()` is derived, not written down, and section 7 asserts it equals the cap.

### The weights are grid searched, not chosen

77 seeded seasons at 34 clubs across every league size, every season scored under BOTH rules,
against three targets at once: hold the median where it was, raise agreement with the board, lower
agreement with stature.

| | OLD | NEW |
|---|---|---|
| median score | 63 | 63 |
| correlation with the board's verdict grade | 0.312 | 0.482 |
| correlation with the club's preview XI | 0.776 | 0.314 |
| correlation with board objectives hit | 0.132 | 0.577 |
| mean, grade A | 65.8 | 82.1 |
| mean, grade B | 67.2 | 69.4 |
| mean, grade C | 58.4 | 48.8 |
| mean, grade D | 28.8 | 34.5 |

**The old rule could not even order A above B.** Holding the median mattered because
`public.global_leaderboard()` pays `100.0 * day_best / max_score` and the denominator lives in the
database, so a rule that simply scored lower would quietly cut every Club Manager player's earning
rate. **Emitting 0..100 instead of 0..130 would have cut it by 23 percent forever.**

### The scale must stay 0..130

Measured from the live database on 2026-09-16: `game_score_caps.max_score` for `club-manager` is
**130**, with **185,460 rows** recorded against it across 1,078 players, p50 33, p90 77, p99 109,
and 114 rows at 130. `game_denominators` is a VIEW over that base table. Changing the emitted
range without changing the row is a silent rescale of every player's history.

### The monotone law

`global_leaderboard()` ranks on `max(least(score, max_score))` per player per game per DAY, and
`recordActivity` pings `currentSeasonScore` after EVERY match from three sites in
`useClubManager.ts`. That is why there are 172 rows per player. So the day's best reading is what
counts, and a score that can FALL pays a player for their luckiest afternoon rather than their
season. A points per game rate reads 3.00 after one opening win and can only decline, which is why
a rate, a league position snapshot, an expectation overshoot and a verdict grade are all
STRUCTURALLY wrong for this game however well they read on a results screen.

### Expectation was deliberately not used

It was the obvious answer and it placed second in the design panel. Two reasons, both found by
reading the engine rather than assumed:

- **A custom club's expectation is re-measured from YOUR OWN squad on every load**
  (`registerCustomClub(parsed.customClub, parsed.eraId, squadXIAvg(parsed.squad))`), so weakening
  your squad lowers the bar you are judged against. Measured: the same custom club moves from
  expectation 20 to 7 when every rating goes up 12.
- **A real club's expectation is frozen at the year zero bake** while actual strength projects
  forward with `yearsOn`, so a long save drifts into free overachievement. By season 11 of a
  Premier League save, Sunderland projects 8th against a frozen expectation of 16.

The board objectives term carries the expectation-aware part instead, because the board already
sizes its own demands to the club: it asks a giant for the title and a minnow to stay up.

### How it was designed

A 21 agent workflow. Five read-only agents mapped the engine, four independent designs were
written from deliberately different angles (expectation relative, achievement ledger, the board's
own objectives, difficulty weighted), and three judges scored each through a distinct lens (a 15
year old on a phone, a leaderboard farmer, the engineer who has to fence it). The Achievement
Ledger won **8/8/8 unanimously**, none disqualified. Both flaws all three judges named
independently were fixed before shipping.

## 5. Traps hit in these two rounds. Read this section before touching any of it.

**The ones that cost real time are first.**

### 5.1 Nine green sections that all measure the same KIND of thing find nothing

This happened TWICE, one round apart, and it is the single most important lesson here.

Round 619: nine sections all measured squad RATING, none measured MONEY, and a money printer
turning 43m into 358.81m sat green through all of them.

Round 628: nine sections measured league size neutrality, monotonicity, two correlations, handover
arithmetic, a gate, a ceiling, a numeric range and a save shape. **None of them ever changed a
squad.** So the monotone law was false and every section was green.

**Before calling a harness done, ask what all of its sections fail to measure.**

### 5.2 The monotone law was FALSE and the section guarding it was blind

`objectiveStatuses` recomputes the youth objective from the CURRENT squad
(`career.squad.filter(p => p.age <= 21 && (p.apps ?? 0) > 0)`), and four of the five board asks in
`clubManagerBoardAsks.ts` read the squad the same way. So a tick could come back OFF. Selling a
man, loaning him out or paying him off took **6 points off a live score** through three buttons
the game really has. Measured: Ajax 43 to 37, Le Havre 17 to 11, and a season forked at week 20
finishing **58 if you kept the player and 48 if you paid him off**.

Section 2 reported "0 of them lower" because it played every career hands off.

**Fix:** the board term lands ONLY at the final whistle (`seasonDone`), where the board itself
settles the card, so there is no sequence of readings to fall through and the score agrees with
the tick list printed beside it on the same screen. Section 2 now buys, sells and pays players off
with a FLOOR on the number of removals, so it cannot go back to passing by never doing the thing
that broke it. Control: `CM_SCORE_CONTROL=liveobjectives` reproduces it on demand and fires at
"Stuttgart week 21 after paying a man off: 21 to 15".

### 5.3 An unknown club name does NOT throw, it becomes a Premier League club

`clubDefFor` returns a flat fallback (tier 4, budget 20, expectation 10) for a name it does not
know, and **`leagueOf` falls back to the PREMIER LEAGUE**. So `Midtjylland` (the real name is
`FC Midtjylland`) silently became an invented Premier League club inside the very run that set
Round 628's weights. The weights were reconfirmed on the rerun and held.

`simClubManagerScore` now refuses to start if any club it names is not in a playable league.
**Any harness that names clubs should do the same.** `simClubManagerFreeAgents` has the same probe
for the same reason, added when `'VfB Stuttgart'` (the registry says `'Stuttgart'`) produced a
career with a fixture list involving nobody and a season that ended in three calls.

### 5.4 A control that changes nothing reports green for the wrong reason

`simClubManagerScore`'s first draft bundled its rewritten modules from the SYSTEM TEMP DIRECTORY,
where `@/lib/...` does not resolve, and its entry imported both modules by ABSOLUTE PATH while the
esbuild aliases only rewrite the `@/lib/` specifier. **Five of the six controls reported green
while changing nothing.** The shadows live under `dist/` (inside the project root, gitignored) and
the entry points at the shadows now.

Round 619 had the sibling of this: `youthonboard`'s anchor broke on an unrelated edit, so the
control failed closed and proved nothing. **Anchors are one line each** for exactly this reason,
and `swap()` exits rather than running when an anchor is gone.

### 5.5 A threshold set inside the noise is a coin toss dressed as a rule

Round 628's stature and board gates were first measured on a 38 season sample where the two arms
OVERLAP (healthy 0.016 to 0.375, old rule 0.331 to 0.653). No threshold separates that. The sample
is 34 clubs and 4 seasons now and the arms separate cleanly. Both gates were then RE-measured
after the whistle gate changed the design, because a threshold inherited across a design change is
not a measured threshold: the board floor of 0.28 was cleared by the OLD RULE at 0.253 on one
seed, which is 0.027 of room.

Current measured bands, three seeds each:

| | new rule | old rule | gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| stature correlation | 0.030 to 0.260 | 0.679 to 0.780 | 0.47 |
| board correlation | 0.669 to 0.746 | 0.095 to 0.253 | 0.45 |

### 5.6 `career.midSeasonStart` is never cleared by the engine

It has exactly TWO writes in the whole repo (the declaration and `startMidSeason`).
`src/pages/ClubManager.tsx` already guards its own takeover banner with `c.season === 1` because
its authors knew the field outlives its season. Round 628's legacy estimate for pre-round takeover
saves is gated on season 1 for the same reason: without the gate, every later season of a takeover
career subtracts a manager who does not exist and the 48 point form term reads zero for roughly
two thirds of the year. **All three design judges found this independently, and it is invisible at
the ends, so it would have shipped.** Control: `nolegacygate`.

### 5.7 A legacy estimate must be a CONSTANT, not a rate

The first version estimated the previous manager's points as `pts * played / leaguePlayed`, a live
rate. That estimate RISES as you lose, so thirteen straight defeats read a growing share of the
form term. It pays a manager for defeats. It is a fixed `LEGACY_PPG = 1.35` now, which cannot move
with your results.

And zeroing what you cannot know is NOT neutral: the legacy branch zeroed `cupRank`, `euroRank`
and `objectivesDone`, which credited the new manager with the previous manager's cup run, European
run and board ticks, about 15 points of 130 on average and 40 at worst. Those three scale by the
share of the season actually managed now. Control: `legacyfreecup`.

### 5.8 Smaller ones, all real

- **The screen and the code drift apart silently.** The help copy stated the OLD formula word for
  word, and nothing tied its numbers to the constants, so the weights could be retuned and the
  screen would go on quoting the old ones. `simClubManagerScore` section 10 reads the numbers from
  the module and from the JSX and compares them. Control: `copydrift`.
- **Raw terms beside a clamped total.** The five terms are worth 154 between them and the scale
  stops at 130, so the season end breakdown could visibly add up to more than the number printed
  above it. It says "N earned, capped at 130" when that happens.
- **A save shape check on an empty career proves nothing.** Section 9 compared an absent handover
  to a null one on a raw `startCareer`, where both read 0, so it passed for ANY implementation. It
  runs on a career with points on the board now.
- **A guard that cannot fail.** Section 1's "not every league size was scored" compared a counter
  incremented unconditionally inside its own loops against the product of their lengths.
- **The `dist` shadow leaked** on an abnormal exit. It is a `process.on('exit')` hook now,
  registered before any section can throw.
- **`handover` out of localStorage is player editable.** A record claiming more games played than
  the table has records of would shrink the denominator and hand out the whole form term for one
  win. Both `pts` and `played` are clamped against what the table itself says happened.
- **The container restarted mid-workflow** and killed a 21 agent run. The transcript survived under
  `~/.claude/projects/.../subagents/workflows/<run>/journal.jsonl`, and
  `Workflow({scriptPath, resumeFromRunId})` replayed the five completed map agents from cache and
  only re-ran the design phase. Worth knowing: commit early, and resume rather than restart.
