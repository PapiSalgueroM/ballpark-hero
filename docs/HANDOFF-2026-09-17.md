Caveat on that measurement, stated because it is a real limit: all 30 signings reachable in

### The three defects, all measured before a line was written

**One, it read the club.** One season at twelve clubs with management held IDENTICAL (nobody
touched anything), so every point of spread is stature. Scores 24 to 90; correlation between a
club's preview XI rating and its season score **0.851**. Bayern finished 8th of 18 on grade F
having hit 0 of 7 board objectives and scored 55, while Sevilla finished 13th on grade C having
hit 3 of 8 and scored 46. The F beat the C.

**Two, the 130 scale did not fit the leagues.** Max league points runs 54 in the 10 club
SuperSport HNL to **138** in the 24 club Championship, against one flat cap. A perfect Croatian
season could never reach the ceiling; the Championship passed it on league points alone.

**Three, relegation paid.** Left alone eight seasons, Sunderland scored 43 finishing 18th in the
Premier League, went down, won the Championship and scored **124**, against 78 for the side that
won the Premier League.

### What it is

A ledger in `src/lib/clubManagerScore.ts`, PURE and importing nothing from `clubManager.ts`, so a
harness can drive it with synthetic inputs and no engine bundle. Every term is a SHARE of what
was available, so league size cancels: league form 48, the title 28 (scaled by the share of the
season you managed), a cup run up to 24, a European run up to 24, board objectives 6 each to 30.

**48 + 28 + 24 + 30 is exactly 130**, the ceiling with no European run, so the five leagues with
no Champions League route can still reach the top. Derived by `ceilingWithoutEurope()` and
asserted by section 7, not written down twice.

### The weights are grid searched

77 seeded seasons at 34 clubs, every season scored under both rules.

| | OLD | NEW |
|---|---|---|
| median score | 63 | 63 |
| correlation with the board's verdict grade | 0.312 | 0.482 |
| correlation with the club's preview XI | 0.776 | 0.314 |
| correlation with board objectives hit | 0.132 | 0.577 |

The old rule could not order grade A above grade B. Holding the median mattered because
`global_leaderboard()` pays `100.0 * day_best / max_score` and the denominator lives in the
database, so a rule that simply scored lower would cut every player's earning rate. Emitting
0..100 instead of 0..130 would have cut it 23 percent forever.

## 3. Traps. The first one is the one that cost a day.

### 3.1 A round claim on a branch is not a claim

See section 1. Both lanes built the same feature for a day. The workboard is only load bearing
when it is on main.

### 3.2 Nine green sections that all measure the same KIND of thing find nothing

This happened twice in this lane, one round apart.

Round 619: nine sections all measured squad RATING, none measured MONEY, and a money printer
turning 43m into 358.81m sat green through all of them.

Round 633: nine sections measured league size neutrality, monotonicity, two correlations,
handover arithmetic, a gate, a ceiling, a range and a save shape. **None of them ever changed a
squad.** So the monotone law was false and every section was green.

**Before calling a harness done, ask what all of its sections fail to measure.**

### 3.3 The monotone law was FALSE and the section guarding it was blind

`objectiveStatuses` recomputes the youth objective from the CURRENT squad, and four of the five
board asks read the squad the same way, so a tick can come back OFF. Releasing, selling or loaning
out one of those players took **6 points off a live score**. Measured: Ajax 43 to 37, Le Havre 17
to 11, and a season forked at week 20 finishing 58 if you kept the player and 48 if you released
him.

**Fix:** the board term lands ONLY at the final whistle, where the board itself settles the card.
Section 2 releases, sells and loans players now, with a FLOOR on the number of removals so it
cannot go back to passing by never doing the thing that broke it. Control:
`CM_SCORE_CONTROL=liveobjectives`.

### 3.4 An unknown club name does NOT throw, it becomes a Premier League club

`clubDefFor` returns a flat fallback for a name it does not know, and **`leagueOf` falls back to
the PREMIER LEAGUE**. So `Midtjylland` (the real name is `FC Midtjylland`) silently became an
invented Premier League club inside the run that set this round's weights. The weights were
reconfirmed on the rerun and held. `simClubManagerScore` now refuses to start if any club it names
is not in a playable league. Any harness that names clubs should do the same.

### 3.5 A control that changes nothing reports green for the wrong reason

The first draft bundled its rewritten modules from the system temp directory, where `@/lib/...`
does not resolve, and its entry imported both modules by ABSOLUTE PATH while the esbuild aliases
only rewrite the `@/lib/` specifier. **Five of the six controls reported green while changing
nothing.** The shadows live under `dist/` now (inside the project root, gitignored) and the entry
points at the shadows.

### 3.6 A threshold set inside the noise is a coin toss dressed as a rule

The gates were first measured on a 38 season sample where the two arms OVERLAP. The sample is 34
clubs and 4 seasons now. They were then RE-measured after the whistle gate changed the design,
because a threshold inherited across a design change is not a measured threshold: the board floor
of 0.28 was cleared by the OLD RULE at 0.253 on one seed.

### 3.7 `career.midSeasonStart` is never cleared by the engine

Two writes in the whole repo. `src/pages/ClubManager.tsx` already guards its own takeover banner
with `c.season === 1` because its authors knew the field outlives its season. The legacy estimate
is gated on season 1 for the same reason. Control: `nolegacygate`.

### 3.8 A legacy estimate must be a CONSTANT, not a rate

A rate estimate RISES as you lose, so it pays a manager for defeats. It is a fixed
`LEGACY_PPG = 1.35` now. And zeroing what you cannot know is NOT neutral: the legacy branch zeroed
the previous manager's cup run, European run and board ticks, which credited them to the new
manager. They scale by the share actually managed now. Control: `legacyfreecup`.

### 3.9 Smaller ones, all real

- The screen and the code drift apart silently. Section 10 reads the numbers from the module and
  from the JSX and compares them. Control: `copydrift`.
- The five terms are worth 154 and the scale stops at 130, so the season end breakdown could add
  up to more than the number above it. It says "N earned, capped at 130" when that happens.
- A save shape check on an empty career proves nothing: absent and null both read 0, so it passed
  for ANY implementation. It runs on a career with points on the board now.
- The `dist` shadow leaked on an abnormal exit. It is a `process.on('exit')` hook now.
- `handover` out of localStorage is player editable, so `pts` and `played` are clamped against
  what the table itself says happened.
- **Verify a pre-existing failure, do not assume it.** The four failing component tests were
  proven pre-existing by running them against a `git archive` of `origin/main` with a symlinked
  `node_modules`, then unlinking the symlink WITHOUT following it (CLAUDE.md's junction trap).
