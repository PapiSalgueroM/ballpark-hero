   speeches live in the shared `src/lib/careerAwardsNight.ts` and `src/components/career/AwardsNightCard.tsx`,
   bound for soccer by `SOCCER_BALLON_DOR`, `SOCCER_BDOR_SPEECHES` and `SOCCER_WORLD_CUP_SPEECHES` in
   `src/lib/soccerCareerEngine.ts`. The US careers can bind the same night later with their own descriptor.
2. **The Ballon d'Or speech is back.** It had no buttons from Round 54 on. A won ceremony now offers the
   speeches this save may give (shared `SpeechChoices`) in place of Continue. One pick (`giveBdorSpeech`,
   guarded by `bdorSpeechOpen`: the ceremony screen, a win, the season just played, no speech yet) moves that
   speech's steps, writes one log line, keeps `{ id, line, moved }` on the night, and the card then shows the
   line and what it moved with Continue (`dismissBallonDor`). `applyBdorSpeech` (speech plus advance in one
   step) is unchanged because a dozen harnesses drive careers with it.
3. **The card says what winning does.** It promised "Legacy +20" (win) and "Legacy +5" (podium); the night
   actually moves Market Value +15 and Popularity +20 (win) and Popularity +5 plus the snub storyline flag
   (podium). Since the review the lines print what the night MEASURABLY moved after the clamps (popularity
   caps at 100, and a winner usually sits there), stored on the night as `moved` by the shared settle.
4. **simBallonDorFairness was a coin toss gate, fixed by its statistic.** See below.
5. What's New entry (newest, October 2026).
