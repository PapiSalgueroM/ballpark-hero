Round 161: the transfer table stops being three buttons that say haggle. An offer is a package now: cash up front, add-ons that weigh sixty pence on the pound and genuinely come due in later summers (about two thirds of the time, measured, leading the summer news when they do), a sell-on clause the seller values at about a third of face and then actually collects off the top when you resell the man, and a part-exchange player who leaves with the deal and cannot be bought back. One dealPackageValue function feeds the offer, the acceptance, the bidding wars and the on-screen preview, so the screen and the table can never disagree, and Meet Ask offers less cash when the structure covers the gap, which is the entire point of structuring a deal. The market grew his filters too: exact positions, left wing against right wing, age bands, price bands, the selling club's league, four sorts, on top of name search. The new harness pinned the old behavior byte for byte for anyone offering plain cash, and its first run caught the summer rollover eating the add-on news, which is what a first run is for.
