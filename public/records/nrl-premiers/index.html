NRL/NSWRL premiers by year

Every top grade rugby league premiership since 1908. 1997 lists both premiers because the game split that year, Newcastle in the ARL and Brisbane in Super League.

No premiership is shown for 2007 or 2009. Melbourne’s titles from those seasons were stripped for salary cap breaches and remain vacant in the official record, so they stay vacant here too.

  • 116 years listed, 1908 to 2025. That is 117 premierships in all, because some years have more than one.
  • Earliest year listed: South Sydney Rabbitohs (1908).
  • Latest year listed: Brisbane Broncos (2025).
  • 20 different club names appear on the list, 1908 to 2025.

    How we counted: each line in the year by year tables counts for the club name exactly as the table writes it, so a club the table writes two ways counts separately under each. The table does not always use the name of the day, so a renamed club may sit under one name or several. 1997 has more than one line, and every club named in that year gets one premiership. Nothing is listed for 2007 and 2009, so no premiership is counted for those years.
