Brownlow Medal winners by year

The VFL/AFL's fairest and best, as voted by the field umpires, every year since 1924. No medal was awarded from 1942 to 1945.

Where a count ended level, every medallist from that year is listed, including the 1930 count settled retrospectively in 1989 and the 2012 medal reallocated in 2016. Older club names appear where the table has them: Footscray, South Melbourne, the Brisbane Bears of 1996.

  • 98 years listed, 1924 to 2025. That is 112 medals in all, because some years have more than one.
  • Earliest year listed: Edward Greeves (1924).
  • Latest year listed: Matt Rowell (2025).
  • 91 different player names appear on the list, 1924 to 2025.

Nothing is listed for 1942 to 1945, so no medal is counted for those years.
