Season by season

1955-56 to 1990-91: Straight knockout, home and away

European Cup

Sixteen invited clubs in the first season, then the champions of each national league. Every round before the final was a two legged tie decided on aggregate, and the final was one match at a venue fixed in advance.

  • The first edition, 1955-56, was won by Real Madrid, 4-3 against Reims at the Parc des Princes in Paris.
  • Three times in this era the final landed on a finalist's own ground: Real Madrid won at the Bernabeu in 1957, Inter won at San Siro in 1965, and Roma lost to Liverpool on penalties at the Stadio Olimpico in 1984.
  • The 1974 final is the only one ever replayed: Bayern Munich and Atletico Madrid drew 1-1 after extra time in Brussels, and Bayern won the replay 4-0 two days later at the same ground.

    • 1991-92: The first group stage

    Thirty two clubs entered the first round; eight survived two knockout rounds. The eight survivors were split into two groups of four, played home and away, and the two group winners met in the final with no semi-finals.

  • Barcelona beat Sampdoria 1-0 after extra time at Wembley, the first final reached through a group.

    • 1992-93: Renamed, same shape

    Champions League

    Eight clubs in two groups of four after the knockout rounds. The two group winners went straight to the final again.

  • The Champions League name arrived this season, at first only for the group stage. Marseille beat Milan 1-0 in Munich.

    • 1993-94: Semi-finals return, as single matches

    Eight clubs in two groups of four after the knockout rounds. The top two of each group went to one off semi-finals, each hosted by a group winner, and the winners met in the final.

  • Milan beat Barcelona 4-0 in Athens.

    • 1994-95 to 1996-97: Sixteen clubs, four groups, quarter-finals

    Sixteen clubs in the group stage. Four groups of four, the top two of each into two legged quarter-finals and semi-finals, then a one match final.

  • Ajax beat Milan 1-0 in Vienna in the first season of this shape, 1994-95, and the shape held for three seasons.

    • 1997-98 to 1998-99: Twenty four clubs, six groups, runners-up let in

    Twenty four clubs in the group stage, including the runners-up of eight domestic leagues for the first time. Six groups of four; the six winners and the two best runners-up went to the quarter-finals.

  • This is where the champions only competition ended, and the name stopped being literally true.
  • Real Madrid beat Juventus 1-0 in Amsterdam in 1998, and Manchester United came from a goal down in stoppage time to beat Bayern Munich 2-1 in Barcelona in 1999.

    • 1999-2000 to 2002-03: Thirty two clubs and a second group stage

    Thirty two clubs in the first group stage, with up to four from the strongest leagues. Eight groups of four, the top two into a second group stage of four groups of four, and the top two of those into the quarter-finals.

  • Third placed clubs from the first stage dropped into the UEFA Cup.
  • Real Madrid beat Valencia 3-0 in Paris in the first season of this format, and the double group stage ran for four seasons.

    • 2003-04 to 2023-24: Thirty two clubs, eight groups, a round of 16

    Thirty two clubs in the group stage. Eight groups of four, the top two into a round of 16, then two legged quarter-finals and semi-finals and a one match final.

  • Porto beat Monaco 3-0 in Gelsenkirchen in the first season of this shape, and it lasted 21 seasons.
  • The 2019-20 season finished as a one city tournament in Lisbon: single match quarter-finals and semi-finals behind closed doors, and Bayern Munich beat Paris Saint-Germain 1-0 in the final.
  • Away goals stopped counting double from 2021-22, so a tie level after two legs now goes to extra time and then penalties.

    • 2024-25 onward: Thirty six clubs in one league table

    Thirty six clubs. One table: each club plays eight different opponents, four at home and four away. The top eight go straight to the round of 16, ninth to 24th play a two legged knockout play-off for the other eight places, and 25th and below are out, with no drop into the Europa League.

  • UEFA calls it the league phase. From the play-offs to the semi-finals the ties are two legged, and the final is still one match at a venue picked well in advance.

    • The away goals rule

    UEFA first used the away goals rule in 1965, in the Cup Winners' Cup, and for most of this competition's life a tie level on aggregate went to whichever side had scored more away from home. It was abolished in every UEFA club competition from 2021-22. A tie level after two legs now goes to extra time and then penalties, and a goal scored away in extra time counts the same as one at home.

    Questions people ask

    When did the Champions League start?

    The competition started in 1955-56 as the European Cup, sixteen invited clubs playing two legged knockout ties with a one match final. The Champions League name arrived in 1992-93, at first only for the group stage, and the shape it named had already been played once, in 1991-92.

    When did the group stage begin, and when did it end?

    The first group stage was 1991-92: two groups of four whose winners met in the final. Groups grew to four in 1994-95, six in 1997-98 and eight in 1999-2000, with a second group stage from 1999-2000 to 2002-03 and a round of 16 in its place from 2003-04. The last group stage was 2023-24; since 2024-25 there is one league table of 36 clubs.

    When did away goals stop counting double?

    From 2021-22, in every UEFA club competition. A tie level after two legs now goes to extra time and then penalties. UEFA had used the rule since 1965.

    How does the league phase work?

    Thirty six clubs sit in one table. Each plays eight different opponents, four at home and four away. The top eight go straight to the round of 16, ninth to 24th play a two legged play-off for the other eight places, and 25th to 36th go out with no Europa League place. From the play-offs to the semi-finals the ties are two legged, and the final is one match.

    Why does the modern Club Manager save play groups instead of the league phase?

    Because the engine does not play the league phase yet, and we would rather say so than pretend. The 2026-27 start is the stand in: eight groups of four into the quarter-finals, two legged ties, no away goals rule. the real 2026-27 competition runs thirty six clubs in one league table, which the engine does not play yet. The 2015-16, 2010-11, 2005-06 starts play the real format of their own seasons.
