Questions people ask before they start

Is any of it paid? No. Every game on this site is free and there is nothing to unlock, no energy meter and no premium tier. Ads pay for the hosting.

Do I need an account? No. Everything plays signed out, your progress is kept in your own browser, and you appear on the world leaderboard without signing up for anything. An account only exists so a save can follow you to another device.

Does it work on a phone? Yes. It is a website, not an app, so there is nothing to install and nothing to update. Every game is built to be played with a thumb first.

Do the daily puzzles reset? Yes, at the same moment for everyone, so the board you get is the board everybody else gets that day. The career and management sims are not daily at all: they are long saves you come back to.

Where does the history come from? The champion tables are read out of The Record Books, checked against the official record before anything is allowed to use them. Seasons that were never played are missing on purpose and stripped titles stay vacant with a note saying why, because a quiz built on tidied up history teaches you the wrong thing.
