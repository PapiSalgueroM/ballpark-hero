    Cookie Policy

    Last updated: October 22, 2026

    Cookies and storage

    DoUKnowBall uses browser storage for game progress, scores, streaks, settings and your cookie choices. Account features also use our sign-in service. Some gameplay data is sent to our database as explained in the Privacy Policy. You can play without an account and with optional advertising off.

    Your choices

    Advertising and Analytics are separate optional purposes. Both start off. Analytics details are unavailable in this copied control. Advertising stays off if your browser sends Global Privacy Control.

    Reject all keeps optional purposes off. Accept all turns on the optional purposes currently available. Save choices applies your available selections. We ask again after 180 days. That period applies to your cookie choice, not a promise to delete game saves or provider records after 180 days.

    Open Cookie choices in the footer whenever you want to change your selection.

    Advertising

    If you turn Advertising on and visit a page with an ad slot, the site can request Google AdSense ads. We request non-personalized ads. These may still use cookies and identifiers for delivery, frequency limits, measurement and fraud prevention. They are not anonymous ads. See the Privacy Policy's advertising section and Google's linked privacy information for more detail.

    Turning advertising off

    Open Cookie choices, turn Advertising off and choose Save choices. We stop future optional loading and clear matching optional cookies our code can access. We normally reload to stop already-loaded scripts. If your choice cannot be saved or a game has unsaved progress, a notice asks you to refresh when it is safe; already-loaded code may continue until then.

    Withdrawal does not erase information already received by a provider or remove cookies on another site or outside our code's access.

    Browser controls and other requests

    You can also clear cookies and stored site data in your browser settings. This can remove local career saves, preferences and your guest handle. Clearing local data does not by itself delete database or provider records.

    Hosting services, account/game services, Google Fonts and FlagCDN can receive technical requests as described in the Privacy Policy. Pausing Google Analytics does not turn off ordinary hosting logs or hosting-provider usage information.

    Cloudflare helps protect our hosted site from automated traffic. It can set a security cookie called __cf_bm. Cloudflare says the cookie holds encrypted bot-detection information and expires after 30 minutes of continuous inactivity. It is separate from optional Advertising and Analytics. See Cloudflare's cookie information.

    For privacy or deletion requests, use the contact details and rights process in the Privacy Policy.
